# Rights and provenance

This package clean-room implements the LCP-accelerated suffix-array search procedure summarized in Srinivas Aluru, “Text Indexing,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_422`, attributed to Manber and Myers.

The chapter and cited research are copyrighted. This package reproduces no source code, prose, or figures and asserts no source-code license. The suffix-array builder is a bounded local preprocessing choice; the benchmarked query profile preserves the chapter's LCP endpoint/midpoint recurrence.
