# Rights and provenance

The blind-sort discovery entry is NIST's [Dictionary of Algorithms and Data
Structures](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/blindSort.html). The method is
described in Manzini and Ferragina, “Engineering a Lightweight Suffix Array
Construction Algorithm,” *Algorithmica* 40(1), 33–50 (2004), [DOI
10.1007/s00453-004-1094-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-004-1094-1), Section
3.1; the authors also provide an [ESA 2002 version](https://people.unipmn.it/manzini/papers/esa02.pdf).

The sources provide algorithm descriptions and attribution only. These ten
ports, the bounded all-suffix contract, workload, references, and prose are
clean-room work. No source code, figure, or prose was copied.
