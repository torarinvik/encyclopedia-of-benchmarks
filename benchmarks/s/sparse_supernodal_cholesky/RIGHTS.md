# Sources and reuse

The [SPLASH-2 repository](https://github.com/staceyson/splash2) identifies sparse Cholesky as a benchmark family. Its code and input data were not copied into this package.

[The study of right-looking supernodal Cholesky by Karsavuran et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13090) describes the numerical algorithm. The article is distributed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. This package uses the algorithmic ideas only; its implementation, contract, and fixtures were independently written, and do not reproduce article text or code. The profile does not reproduce the SPLASH-2 scheduler or symbolic analysis.
