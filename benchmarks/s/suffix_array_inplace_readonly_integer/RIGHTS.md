# Rights and provenance

The algorithm was independently implemented from the descriptions in Zhize
Li, Jian Li, and Hongwei Huo, “Optimal In-Place Suffix Sorting,”
*Information and Computation*, [DOI 10.1016/j.ic.2021.104818](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ic.2021.104818),
[arXiv:1610.08305](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.08305), especially Section 4
on read-only integer alphabets. The OI Wiki
[Optimal In-place Suffix Sort](https://en.oi-wiki.org/string/sa-optimal-inplace/)
page supplied discovery
and a secondary explanation. No source code, benchmark inputs, or prose was
copied. The ports, references, and workload fixtures are clean-room.
