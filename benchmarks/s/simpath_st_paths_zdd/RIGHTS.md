# Rights and provenance

The benchmark is based on the method descriptions in Chapter 734 of the
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, [“Counting by ZDD”](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_734),
and Donald E. Knuth, *The Art of Computer Programming*, Vol. 4A, §7.1.4. The
frontier/mate-state details are cross-checked against Iwashita's Hokkaido
University [technical report](https://www-alg.ist.hokudai.ac.jp/~thomas/TCSTR/tcstr_13_69/tcstr_13_69.pdf).
Knuth's [program index](https://cs.stanford.edu/~knuth/programs.html) is used
for bibliographic/source identification only.

No upstream source code, examples, fixtures, or prose are copied or
redistributed. The bounded input contract, two references, workload, static
inventory helper, and ten language ports are clean-room implementations of the
published algorithmic ideas.
