The algorithm is attributed to Craig G. Nevill-Manning and Ian H. Witten,
“Identifying Hierarchical Structure in Sequences: A linear-time algorithm,”
*Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research* 7 (1997), 67–82,
[paper PDF](https://ml.cms.waikato.ac.nz/publications/1997/NM-IHW-Hierarch97.pdf),
and Craig G. Nevill-Manning, *Inferring Sequential Structure* (1996),
[thesis PDF](https://ml.cms.waikato.ac.nz/publications/1996/NevMan-Thesis.pdf).

This package uses the published algorithmic ideas and attribution only. It does
not copy implementation code, figures, or prose. The deterministic bounded
contract, primary and independent references, workload, documentation, and ten
language ports are original clean-room work. Rights in the cited publications
remain with their respective authors and publishers.
