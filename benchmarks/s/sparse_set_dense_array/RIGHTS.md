# Rights and provenance

The package implements the dense/sparse-array representation described by
Preston Briggs and Linda Torczon in “An Efficient Representation for Sparse
Sets,” *ACM Letters on Programming Languages and Systems* 2(1–4), 59–69
(1993), [DOI 10.1145/176454.176484](https://doi.org/10.1145/176454.176484).
An available [paper copy](https://www.dcs.gla.ac.uk/~pat/ads2/papers/sparseSets.pdf)
is cited for its mathematical data-structure description.

Only the algorithmic representation and operation semantics are used. No
published code, prose, or examples are copied. The ports and references were
written specifically for this bounded benchmark. The cited publication and
paper copy retain their own rights and terms.
