# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic definitions in this package follow Chapter 585 of the
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms* and §2 of de Berg, Haverkort, Thite, and Toma,
“Star-quadtrees and guard-quadtrees: I/O-efficient indexes for fat
triangulations and low-density planar subdivisions,”
[doi:10.1016/j.comgeo.2009.11.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comgeo.2009.11.001).
The primary paper is available from the authors at
<https://tildesites.bowdoin.edu/~ltoma/papers/journal-2008-quad.pdf>.

No source code was copied. The implementations, references, bounded input
profile, cell/triangle serialization, workload, and documentation were written
independently. No license is asserted over the cited mathematical method or
the clean-room package. The cited paper and book retain their respective
copyrights and terms.
