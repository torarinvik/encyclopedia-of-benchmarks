# Rights and provenance

The recurrence and S-fraction output mapping follow NIST DLMF
[§3.10(ii), Eqs. 3.10.8–3.10.10](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.10#E8). This package
defines a deterministic finite-field profile of those algebraic recurrences.
Its contract, modular field implementation, references, fixtures, and language
ports are clean-room work.

No source code, comments, prose, examples, or tests have been copied. The
DLMF's terms govern its text and presentation; no license for copied material
is claimed.
