# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation of the bounded suffix-tray
pattern-query profile. The algorithmic source is Cole, Kopelowitz, and
Lewenstein, “Suffix Trays and Suffix Trists: Structures for Faster Text
Indexing,” ICALP 2006, §3.1–3.3, DOI
[10.1007/11786986_32](https://doi.org/10.1007/11786986_32). Chapter context
comes from Aluru, “Text Indexing,” Chapter 422 of the *Encyclopedia of
Algorithms*, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_422](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_422).

No source code, prose, figures, example inputs, or tests were copied. The
bounded contract, direct suffix-tree adapter, workload fixtures, references,
and language ports were independently authored. The chapter's broader text
indexing survey and the paper's online suffix-trist operations are outside
this profile.
