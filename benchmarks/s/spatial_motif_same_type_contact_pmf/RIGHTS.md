# Source and rights

The same-type spatial-contact hypergeometric distribution is attributed to
Jie Liang and Ronald Jackups, “Sequence and Spatial Motif Discovery in Short
Sequence Fragments,” Encyclopedia of Algorithms, DOI
10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_601 (`OEBPS/AChap601.html`). The chapter is used to
identify the probability mass formula and expectation. No source-code license
grant is asserted. These implementations are clean-room ports and do not copy
chapter prose or source code.
