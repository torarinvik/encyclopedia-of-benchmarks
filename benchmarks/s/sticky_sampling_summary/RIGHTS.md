# Provenance

The algorithmic idea is from Gurmeet Singh Manku and Rajeev Motwani,
[*Approximate Frequency Counts over Data Streams*](https://www.vldb.org/conf/2002/S10P03.pdf)
(VLDB 2002), section 4.1. The paper permits certain noncommercial copying under
stated conditions and reserves other copying. This package independently
authors its contract, code, and fixtures; no paper text or examples are reused.
