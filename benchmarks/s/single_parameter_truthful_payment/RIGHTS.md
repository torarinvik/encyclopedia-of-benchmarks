# Rights and provenance

This package clean-room implements the bounded integer-step profile of the Archer–Tardos payment formula summarized in Moshe Babaioff, “Truthful Mechanisms for One-Parameter Agents,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_436`.

The chapter and cited research are copyrighted. This package reproduces no source code, prose, or figures and asserts no license over the source material. It covers the payment-characterization formula only, not the chapter's scheduling or auction mechanisms.
