# Rights and provenance

Algorithm attribution: Rainer Schuler, “An algorithm for the satisfiability
problem of formulas in conjunctive normal form,” *Journal of Algorithms* 54
(2005), DOI 10.1016/j.jalgor.2004.04.012, Section 2. The algorithm is included
in *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 133, DOI
10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_133. The PPZ subroutine is attributed to Paturi,
Pudlák, and Zane, “Satisfiability Coding Lemma,” FOCS 1997, DOI
10.1109/SFCS.1997.646146.

The papers and chapter provide algorithm attribution and mathematical
specification only. The language ports, references, and workload were written
for this repository. No source code, chapter prose, figures, or third-party
workload data is included, and no license over the source works is asserted.

The benchmark fixes supplied random choices so one trace is reproducible. This
does not imply or measure the source algorithm's probability of success or
expected running time.
