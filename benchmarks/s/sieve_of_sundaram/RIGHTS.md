# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation of a mathematical prime-sieving
method. Its algorithm description is supported by Ghidarcea and Popescu,
“GPGPU and Sieve of Sundaram,” UPB Scientific Bulletin, Series C, 86(4),
2024, pp. 31–42, [publisher-hosted PDF](https://www.scientificbulletin.upb.ro/rev_docs_arhiva/fullb1a_720372.pdf),
and the peer-reviewed review [Prime Number Sieving—A Systematic Review with
Performance Analysis](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4893/17/4/157). The method's
original attribution is V. Ramaswami Aiyar, “Sundaram's Sieve for Prime
Numbers,” The Mathematics Student 2(2), 1934, p. 73
([catalog entry](https://books.google.fr/books?focus=searchwithinvolume&hl=fr&id=uKjxAAAAMAAJ&q=Sundaram%27Sieve&redir_esc=y)).

Only the mathematical marking rule and prime mapping are used. The contract,
workload, references, and ten language ports were written for this repository;
no source code, prose, examples, or test data were copied.
