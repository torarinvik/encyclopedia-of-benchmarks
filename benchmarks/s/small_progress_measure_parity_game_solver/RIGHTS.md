# Rights and provenance

This clean-room package implements the mathematical algorithm described by
Marcin Jurdziński in “Small Progress Measures for Solving Parity Games,”
STACS 2000, [DOI 10.1007/3-540-46541-3_24](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-46541-3_24),
and attributed in Willemse and Gazda's *Encyclopedia of Algorithms* Chapter
591, [DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_591](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_591).
Copyright in the papers and chapter remains with their respective publishers.

Only the mathematical algorithm and bibliographic facts were used. No source
code, protected prose, figures, examples, or workload fixtures were copied.
The bounded ties, input encoding, and output encoding are benchmark-profile
choices.
