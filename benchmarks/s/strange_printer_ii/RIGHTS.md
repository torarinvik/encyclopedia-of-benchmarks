# Rights and provenance

The public [LeetCode 1591 title and task definition](https://leetcode.com/problems/strange-printer-ii/)
identifies the one-use-per-color rectangular printing operation. The method's
topological-order component is attributed to A. B. Kahn, “Topological sorting
of large networks,” *Communications of the ACM* 5(11), 558–562 (1962),
[doi:10.1145/368996.369025](https://doi.org/10.1145/368996.369025).

The bounding-box dependency reduction, bounded profile, both references,
workload, documentation, and language ports are clean-room derivations. No
LeetCode source code, editorial, examples, test cases, or problem prose is
copied.
