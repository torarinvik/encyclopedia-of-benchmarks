# Rights and attribution

This package is a clean-room implementation of the sink-side estimation
procedure in Fig. 2 of “Randomized Energy Balance Algorithms in Sensor
Networks,” by Pierre Leone, Sotiris Nikoletseas, and José Rolim, *Encyclopedia
of Algorithms*, DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_323. The chapter is copyrighted;
no text, figures, or source code are reproduced. Supplied Bernoulli draws and
the accepted-state precondition are benchmark refinements, not probabilistic
or network-level guarantees.
