# Rights and provenance

The recurrence is based on NIST DLMF [§3.10(iii), Eq. 3.10.16](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.10#E16)
and [Eq. 3.10.17](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.10#E17).
The source is cited for algorithmic provenance. The finite-field contract,
right-to-left independent reference, workload, and all ports are independently
written.

No source code, comments, prose, examples, or tests have been copied. The
DLMF's terms govern its text and presentation; this package makes no claim of
a license for copied material.
