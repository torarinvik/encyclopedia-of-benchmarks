# Provenance

The [OI Wiki slope-trick overview](https://oi-wiki.org/dp/opt/slope-trick/)
describes maintaining convex breakpoints in heaps with lazy translation. OI
Wiki lists CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA terms; only the mathematical method is used.
This package's contract, implementations, reference, and eventual fixtures are
independently written; no upstream text, code, or examples are copied.
