# Rights and provenance

The source descriptions are Cristianini and Ricci, “Support Vector Machines,”
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 415, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_415](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_415),
and John C. Platt, “Sequential Minimal Optimization: A Fast Algorithm for
Training Support Vector Machines,” Microsoft Research Technical Report
MSR-TR-98-14 (1998), [official publication page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/sequential-minimal-optimization-a-fast-algorithm-for-training-support-vector-machines/)
and [technical report PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/tr-98-14.pdf).

The package independently expresses the algorithm and uses a deterministic,
finite-pass benchmark contract. It does not include source code, copied prose,
figures, or upstream workload data. The book and report remain under their
respective copyrights; no redistribution license is claimed.
