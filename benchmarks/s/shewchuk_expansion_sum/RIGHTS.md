# Rights and provenance

This package contains clean-room implementations of the `Two-Sum` and
`Grow-Expansion` floating-point expansion methods described by Jonathan Richard
Shewchuk in “Adaptive Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic and Fast Robust
Geometric Predicates,” *Discrete & Computational Geometry* 18(3), 305–363
(1997), [DOI 10.1007/PL00009321](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009321).

Only the mathematical algorithm and its bounded input/output semantics are
used. No third-party implementation, paper prose, or test vectors are copied.
The primary and integer verifier were written specifically for this
repository. The cited publication retains its own rights and terms.
