# Rights and provenance

The exact heterotypic spatial-contact distribution is attributed to Ronald
Jackups Jr. and Jie Liang, “Combinatorial Analysis for Sequence and Spatial
Motif Discovery in Short Sequence Fragments,” *IEEE/ACM Transactions on
Computational Biology and Bioinformatics* 7(3), 524–536 (2010), DOI
[10.1109/TCBB.2008.101](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCBB.2008.101), §II-B.
The method is also summarized in “Sequence and Spatial Motif Discovery in
Short Sequence Fragments,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 601, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_601](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_601).

The papers specify algorithmic behavior and attribution; no source-code
license grant is asserted. The language ports, workload cases, and both
reference implementations are clean-room work. No source code, comments,
prose, or protected fixtures are reproduced.
