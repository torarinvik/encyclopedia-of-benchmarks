# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room bounded replay of the sequential weighted
spanner algorithm attributed to Baswana and Sen. The Encyclopedia chapter and
the cited primary paper are used as algorithmic sources; neither is treated
as a code license. No source code, prose, pseudocode, or figures were copied.
The implementations are original and remain subject to the repository's
terms. Source references are listed in [README.md](README.md).
