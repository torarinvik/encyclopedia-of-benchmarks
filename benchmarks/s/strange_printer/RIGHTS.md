# Rights and provenance

The task definition follows public title metadata and the operation described
by [LeetCode 664, Strange Printer](https://leetcode.com/problems/strange-printer/).
The benchmark remaps characters to a bounded three-symbol alphabet and uses no
LeetCode code, editorial, tests, examples, or prose. The interval recurrence,
independent state-space oracle, workload, documentation, and ten ports are
clean-room work.
