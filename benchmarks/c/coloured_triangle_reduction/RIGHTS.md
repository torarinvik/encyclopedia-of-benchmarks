# Rights and provenance

The Codewars [Coloured Triangles](https://www.codewars.com/kata/5a25ac6ac5e284cfbe000111)
title was used as an idea-index lead only. No kata source, examples, tests, or
prose are copied.

The binomial-coefficient reduction follows the standard linear-operator
expansion `(I+S)^r`; coefficients modulo 3 are computed using Lucas' theorem.
References:

- Édouard Lucas, “Théorie des fonctions numériques simplement périodiques,”
  *American Journal of Mathematics* 1(2), 184–196 (1878),
  [doi:10.2307/2369308](https://doi.org/10.2307/2369308).
- Kenneth S. Davis and William A. Webb, “Lucas' Theorem for Prime Powers,”
  *European Journal of Combinatorics* 11(3), 229–233 (1990),
  [doi:10.1016/S0195-6698(13)80122-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0195-6698(13)80122-9).

The bounded `i64_array_scalar` contract, Lucas implementation, repeated-row
oracle, workload, documentation, and ten ports are clean-room work.
