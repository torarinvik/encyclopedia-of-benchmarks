# Rights and provenance

The Two-Way string-matching algorithm is attributed to Maxime Crochemore and
Dominique Perrin, “Two-way string-matching,” *Journal of the ACM* 38(3),
651–675, 1991, DOI
[10.1145/116825.116845](https://doi.org/10.1145/116825.116845). The article is
copyrighted by ACM. This package uses the published algorithm idea and citation
for provenance only; it does not reproduce article text, figures, source code,
or example data. The ten ports, bounded contract, workload, and oracles are
independently written clean-room work.
