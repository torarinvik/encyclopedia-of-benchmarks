# Source and reuse

The candidate uses mathematical descriptions from Timothy M. Chan's
[1996 paper](https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~suri/cs235/ChanCH.pdf) and the
[KIT geometry notes](https://i11www.iti.kit.edu/_media/teaching/sommer2018/compgeom/psession1.pdf).
The paper is copyrighted. No source code, prose, or fixtures were copied; the
contract, references, language drafts, and workload are independently written.
