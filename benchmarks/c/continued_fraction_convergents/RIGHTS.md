# Rights and provenance

The recurrence comes from the “Convergents” section of the [CP-Algorithms continued-fractions article](https://cp-algorithms.com/algebra/continued-fractions.html#convergents), which is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. This package uses the mathematical recurrence only. Its bounded contract, fixtures, oracle implementations, and ports were independently authored; no upstream code, prose, or examples were copied.

The primary oracle advances the two numerator/denominator recurrences. The independent oracle evaluates each finite prefix from right to left.
