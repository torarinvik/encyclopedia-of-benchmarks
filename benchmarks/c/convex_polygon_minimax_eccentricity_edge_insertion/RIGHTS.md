# Rights and provenance

The benchmark's problem definition and edge-insertion ideas come from Tiow-Seng
Tan's *Optimal Triangulation*, *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_715](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_715),
and Bern, Edelsbrunner, Eppstein, Mitchell, and Tan, *Edge Insertion for
Optimal Triangulations*, DOI
[10.1007/BF02573962](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02573962). The paper is available
from an author-hosted copy at
[people.eecs.berkeley.edu](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jrs/meshpapers/BEEMT.pdf).

Those publications remain under their respective copyrights. This package
contains no copied source code, figures, or extended passages. The bounded
contract, rational eccentricity routine, retessellation procedure, workload,
oracles, and ten language ports were independently written from the published
problem definition and algorithm ideas.
