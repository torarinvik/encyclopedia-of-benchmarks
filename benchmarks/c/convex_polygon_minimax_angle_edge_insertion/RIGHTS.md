# Rights and provenance

- Chapter: Tiow-Seng Tan, “Optimal Triangulation,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*,
  DOI [10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_715](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_715).
- Primary algorithm paper: H. Edelsbrunner, T. S. Tan, and R. Waupotitsch,
  “An O(n² log n) Time Algorithm for the Minmax Angle Triangulation,” *SIAM
  Journal on Scientific Computing* 13(4), 994–1008 (1992), DOI
  [10.1137/0913058](https://doi.org/10.1137/0913058). An author-hosted copy is
  linked from [Tiow-Seng Tan’s publication page](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~tants/).
- The chapter and paper are copyrighted publications. Their descriptions are
  cited as provenance; citation does not grant rights to redistribute their
  text, figures, or code.
- The package uses the min-max-angle objective and edge-insertion algorithm
  description. The bounded contract, fixtures, exact integer comparison,
  references, and ten language ports are clean-room implementations.
- The package does not claim to reproduce the paper's general-point-set
  O(n² log n) implementation. It profiles the fully enumerable convex
  triangle/quadrilateral case and its one-candidate edge-insertion step.
