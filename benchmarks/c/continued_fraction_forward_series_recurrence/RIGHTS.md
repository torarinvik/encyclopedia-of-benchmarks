# Rights and provenance

The recurrence follows NIST DLMF [§3.10(iii), Eq. 3.10.15](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.10#E15).
The equation and its surrounding description are used as algorithmic
provenance. The finite-field contract, references, test workload, and source
ports are independently written.

No source code, comments, prose, examples, or tests have been copied. The
DLMF's terms govern its text and presentation; this package makes no claim of
a license for copied material.
