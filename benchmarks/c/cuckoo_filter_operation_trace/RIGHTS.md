# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic basis is Bin Fan, David G. Andersen, Michael Kaminsky, and
Michael D. Mitzenmacher, “Cuckoo Filter: Practically Better Than Bloom,”
Proceedings of the 10th ACM International Conference on emerging Networking
EXperiments and Technologies (CoNEXT 2014),
[DOI 10.1145/2674005.2674994](https://doi.org/10.1145/2674005.2674994),
especially Sections 3.1–3.3. NIST DADS entries for
[cuckoo hashing](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/cuckooHashing.html) and
[Bloom filters](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/bloomFilter.html) are
discovery sources.

Only the algorithmic ideas are used. No source code, pseudocode wording,
examples, figures, or fixtures are copied or adapted. The supplied
hash/fingerprint descriptor, small deterministic hash family, deterministic
eviction order, trace framing, references, fixtures, and ten ports are
independently authored. The profile makes no probabilistic bound for its
pinned functions or deterministic kick schedule.
