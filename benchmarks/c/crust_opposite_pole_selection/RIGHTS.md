# Rights and provenance

The package is a clean-room implementation of the mathematical pole-selection
rule in Nina Amenta, Marshall Bern, and Manolis Kamvysselis, “A New
Voronoi-Based Surface Reconstruction Algorithm,” SIGGRAPH '98, §4, Algorithm
step 2(a,c), pp. 4–5, DOI
[10.1145/280814.280947](https://doi.org/10.1145/280814.280947), as summarized
in Amenta, *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 713, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_713](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_713).

The sources are cited for mathematical method and attribution only. No source
code, prose, figures, examples, or fixtures were copied. The input fixtures
were authored independently to exercise bounded distance ties, opposite-side
selection, and the no-opposite sentinel. The ten language ports and references
are independently written from the operation contract.

No source-code license grant is asserted for either cited publication. The
repository package and workload contain only the clean-room implementation,
the benchmark ABI, and independently authored test data.
