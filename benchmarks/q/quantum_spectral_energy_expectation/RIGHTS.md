# Rights and provenance

The [Algorithm Archive Quantum Systems chapter](https://www.algorithm-archive.org/contents/quantum_systems/quantum_systems.html)
was used as method context for the Fourier-domain kinetic term, pointwise
potential term, and Hamiltonian expectation reduction. The Archive labels its
code examples MIT and its chapter text CC BY-SA 4.0.

This benchmark is a clean-room implementation based on the mathematical
operation. No source code, chapter text, example, figure, fixture, or test was
copied or adapted. The bounded Q8 contract, FFT implementations, independent
direct-DFT reference, and workloads were authored independently.
