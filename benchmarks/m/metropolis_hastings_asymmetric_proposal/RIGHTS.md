# Rights and provenance

The Algorithm Archive Metropolis chapter was used to discover the named
Metropolis–Hastings extension. The transition method is specified from
Hastings's 1970 paper, “Monte Carlo sampling methods using Markov chains and
their applications,” *Biometrika* 57(1), 97–109,
[DOI 10.1093/biomet/57.1.97](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/57.1.97).

No upstream source code, text, figures, examples, or test data were copied.
The finite-state domain, integer proposal matrix, rational draw encoding,
fixtures, references, and ten language ports are independently authored. The
workload reports deterministic transitions for its supplied tape and makes no
statistical convergence claim.
