# Rights and provenance

This is a clean-room implementation of the interval-graph pair-DAG
construction described in Encyclopedia of Algorithms DOI
10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_721 and Kanté et al., DOI
10.1007/978-3-642-45030-3_32. No upstream source code, book prose, or figures
are copied. The cited publications are algorithm references, not source-code
licenses.
