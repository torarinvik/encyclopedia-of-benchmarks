# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Eyal Even-Dar, “Reinforcement Learning,”
Chapter 341, Section “Policy Iteration,” in *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*,
second edition (Springer, 2016),
[doi:10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_341](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_341).
The section describes evaluation to a policy Bellman fixed point followed by
greedy policy improvement.

Only the mathematical algorithmic description was consulted. The bounded
contract, exact functional-graph evaluation, source ports, references,
fixtures, and explanatory prose are independently written. No book or cited
source code, prose, examples, or figures are included. Source rights remain
with the cited authors and publisher.
