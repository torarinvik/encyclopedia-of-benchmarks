# Rights and provenance

The modified Newton iteration is attributed to NIST's Digital Library of
Mathematical Functions, [§3.8(ii), Newton's rule](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.8#E4).
The text states that when the zero multiplicity is known, multiplying the
Newton correction by that multiplicity restores quadratic convergence.

This benchmark specializes that rule to the polynomial
`f(x)=(x-1)^2(x+2)`, whose positive root at 1 has known multiplicity 2. The
bounded input contract, ten ports, fixtures, and references were authored for
this repository from the mathematical rule. No source code, prose, figures,
or example values were copied. The citation does not imply endorsement or
grant a source-code license.
