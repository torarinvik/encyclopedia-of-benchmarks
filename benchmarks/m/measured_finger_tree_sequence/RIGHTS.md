# Source and rights

The structural reference is OI Wiki's [Finger Tree article](https://oi-wiki.org/ds/finger-tree/), which describes a purely functional sequence with the `Empty`/`Single`/`Deep` form, 1–4 element digits, `Node2`/`Node3`, monoidal measures, and end/append/split operations. The article identifies its content as CC BY-SA 4.0 and also lists SATA terms. The foundational method reference is Hinze and Paterson, [“Finger trees: a simple general-purpose data structure”](https://www.staff.city.ac.uk/~ross/papers/FingerTree.html), DOI 10.1017/S0956796805005769.

The benchmark contract and all ten implementations are independently written. No upstream code, prose, or diagrams are reproduced. The citations identify the data-structure method; this notice does not relicense the cited material.
