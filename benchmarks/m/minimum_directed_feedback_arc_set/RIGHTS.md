# Rights and provenance

The bounded profile follows the vertex-ordering formulation of directed
feedback arc set described by Hans L. Bodlaender, Fedor V. Fomin, Arie M. C. A.
Koster, Dieter Kratsch, and Dimitrios M. Thilikos in “A Note on Exact
Algorithms for Vertex Ordering Problems on Graphs,” *Theory of Computing
Systems* 50 (2012), 420–432, [DOI 10.1007/s00224-011-9312-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00224-011-9312-0).
The open-access [author manuscript](https://bora.uib.no/bora-xmlui/bitstream/handle/1956/4556/A%20Note%20on%20Exact%20Algorithms%20for%20Vertex%20Ordering%20Problems%20on%20Graphs.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)
states the backward-arc equivalence in Lemma 9 and gives the subset-DP
recurrence for additive ordering objectives in Section 3.

The package uses the mathematical formulation only. The ten ports, both
oracles, fixtures, and documentation were written for this bounded benchmark;
no source implementation or prose was copied. The cited publication and PDF
retain their own rights and license terms.
