# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Wendy Myrvold and Frank Ruskey, “Ranking and
Unranking Permutations in Linear Time,” *Information Processing Letters* 79
(2001), 281–284, Algorithm 1, [DOI
10.1016/S0020-0190(01)00141-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0020-0190(01)00141-7).
NIST DADS's [unranking entry](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/unrank.html)
was used to discover the operation and primary citation.

The paper and DADS entry were consulted as algorithm references; no license
grant for their prose or published code was identified. No source code,
comments, examples, fixtures, tests, or prose were copied. The ten ports and
two references are clean-room implementations of the mathematical swap and
inverse-ranking procedures. The independent workload fixtures were derived
separately from the bounded contract.
