# Rights and provenance

This benchmark implements the published MRG32k3a mathematical recurrence
from Pierre L’Ecuyer’s 1999 parameter paper and the 2002 stream-package paper
§1.1. The RosettaCodeData popular-task list at revision
`1d475861d7141ef2fafff293d9339f56e4b0f5db` was consulted only to discover the
algorithm title.

No source code, implementation text, task statements, task examples, or tests
were copied. The benchmark-specific explicit-state ABI, integer output trace,
fixtures, workload, references, and ten language ports were written for this
repository. The cited papers supply algorithm provenance; their licenses are
not asserted here.
