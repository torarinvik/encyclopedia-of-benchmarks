# Provenance and rights

The algorithm is based on the mathematical definition of Minkowski's
question-mark function in Giedrius Alkauskas, “The moments of Minkowski
question mark function: the dyadic period function,” *Glasgow Mathematical
Journal* 52 (2010), 41–64, [DOI 10.1017/S0017089509990152](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0017089509990152).
The equivalent mediant identity is also described by MathWorld's
[Minkowski's Question Mark Function](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/MinkowskisQuestionMarkFunction.html).
RosettaCodeData's pinned score-20 [popular-task title index](https://github.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/blob/1d475861d7141ef2fafff293d9339f56e4b0f5db/popular-tasks)
served only to discover the task title.

No task prose, source code, examples, or tests were copied. The bounded
contract, ten language ports, workload, references, and documentation were
written independently. The cited sources retain their own copyrights and
licenses; only their mathematical descriptions are used here.
