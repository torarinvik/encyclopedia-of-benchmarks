# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is based on NIST DLMF [§3.6(iii), Miller's algorithm](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.6.iii)
and its [normalization equation 3.6.5](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.6#E5). The
bounded finite-field profile and finite normalization functional are explicit
benchmark adaptations. References, fixtures, and source ports are independently
written.

No source code, comments, prose, examples, or tests have been copied. The
DLMF's terms govern its text and presentation; this package makes no claim of
a license for copied material.
