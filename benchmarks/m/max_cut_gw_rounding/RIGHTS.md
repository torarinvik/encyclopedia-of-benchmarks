# Rights and provenance

This benchmark is a clean-room implementation of the Goemans–Williamson
hyperplane-rounding step identified in the “Max Cut” chapter of the
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI
[`10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_219`](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_219).
The chapter is cited only to identify the algorithm. No source code, figures,
tables, or expressive prose from the book are included in this package.
