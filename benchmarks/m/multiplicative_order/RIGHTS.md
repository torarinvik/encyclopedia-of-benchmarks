# Rights and provenance

The reduction criterion follows the relation between primitive roots and element orders in the [CP-Algorithms Primitive Root article](https://cp-algorithms.com/algebra/primitive-root.html), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. This package uses the mathematical method only. Its bounded contract, fixtures, references, and language ports were independently written; no upstream code, examples, or prose were copied.

The primary factors `φ(m)` and tests prime-factor reductions with modular exponentiation. The independent reference enumerates successive powers until the first return to one.
