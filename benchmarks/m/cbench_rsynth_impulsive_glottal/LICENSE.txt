Public Domain Software (from Carnegie Mellon University).

Certain dictionaries have special copyright restrictions, namely "beep".
This is a direct desendant of CUVOLAD (british pronounciation) (as used
by previous releases of rsynth), and so has a more restrictive copyright
than the CMU dictionary.

This package contains no dictionary or speech data and does not use the
separately restricted "beep" dictionary. Its ports are clean-room modified
implementations of the bounded impulsive-source/resonator profile.
