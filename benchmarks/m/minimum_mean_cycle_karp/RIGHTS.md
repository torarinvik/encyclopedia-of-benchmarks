# Rights and provenance

The graph topic was identified through the repository's tracked *Encyclopedia
of Algorithms* crosswalk for “Negative Cycles in Weighted Digraphs.” The
minimum-cycle-mean algorithm is separately attributed to Richard M. Karp,
“A characterization of the minimum cycle mean in a digraph,” *Discrete
Mathematics* 23(3), 1978, pp. 309–311,
[DOI 10.1016/0012-365X(78)90011-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(78)90011-0).

The paper's algorithm characterization is used for attribution and clean-room
implementation only. No paper source code, prose, figures, or example data is
reproduced. The local bounded contract, workload, Karp dynamic program, and
independent simple-cycle enumeration oracle are original to this package.
