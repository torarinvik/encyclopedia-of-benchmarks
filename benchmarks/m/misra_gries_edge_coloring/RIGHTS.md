# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic basis is Misra and Gries, “A Constructive Proof of Vizing's
Theorem,” *Information Processing Letters* 41(3), 131–133 (1992),
https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(92)90041-S. The authors' paper is hosted at
https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~misra/psp.dir/vizing.pdf. KACTL's
https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/graph/EdgeColoring.h
is used as a catalog reference; its header identifies itself as CC0. No
upstream source code or prose was copied. The ten implementations, references,
fixtures, deterministic tie rules, and documentation were written for this
repository from the mathematical description.
