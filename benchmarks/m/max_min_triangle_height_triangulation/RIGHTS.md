# Rights and provenance

This benchmark contains clean-room implementations of a bounded
max-min triangle-height triangulation profile. The problem objective is
described in Tiow-Seng Tan's [Encyclopedia of Algorithms entry, “Optimal
Triangulation”](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_715). The
edge-insertion method is informed by Bern, Edelsbrunner, Eppstein, Mitchell,
and Tan, [“Edge insertion for optimal triangulations”](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02573962),
and Tan's [author-hosted paper](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~tants/Paper/eis.pdf).

Only algorithmic ideas and problem semantics are used. No third-party source
code, benchmark implementation, or prose has been copied into the language
ports or reference implementations. The primary bounded edge-insertion
reference and the independent exhaustive-triangulation reference were
written specifically for this repository.

This file records provenance; it does not grant rights to the cited
third-party publications. Consult their publishers or authors for the
applicable terms of those works.
