# Rights and provenance

The discovery lead was the public LeetCode title “Minimum Cost to Connect Two Groups of Points.” This package defines its own bounded complete bipartite edge-cover profile from the mathematical edge-cover objective: choose a set of weighted edges so every vertex is incident to at least one chosen edge, minimizing the sum of chosen-edge costs.

Hessler and Wästlund's “Edge Cover and Polymatroid Flow Problems” gives the edge-cover objective for complete bipartite graphs. Ferdous, Pothen, and Halappanavar describe the minimum-weight-matching reduction used by the independent reference. The DP recurrence, bounded ABI, fixtures, and all ten ports were authored independently. No LeetCode task text, examples, tests, submissions, source code, or fixtures were copied.

- Discovery title: <https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-cost-to-connect-two-groups-of-points/>
- Edge-cover paper: <https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/EJP-ECP/article/view/846.html>
- Matching-reduction paper: <https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2024.12>
