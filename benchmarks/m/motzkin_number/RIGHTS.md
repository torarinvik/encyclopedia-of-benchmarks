# Rights and provenance

All ports and fixtures are original implementations of the Motzkin recurrence
and its binomial-Catalan identity. The mathematical sequence and its formulas
are not copied source code. No code, prose, or fixtures were taken from the
RosettaCodeData tasks.

The algorithm description is based on Donaghey and Shapiro, “Motzkin Numbers,”
*Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 23(3) (1977), 291–301. The OEIS
entry is used only to cross-reference the sequence values.

- [Donaghey and Shapiro paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(77)90020-6)
- [OEIS A001006](https://oeis.org/A001006)
