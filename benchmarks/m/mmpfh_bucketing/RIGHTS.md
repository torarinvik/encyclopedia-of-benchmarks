# Source and rights

This package is a clean-room implementation of the bounded equal-size-bucket
construction described in:

Paolo Boldi and Sebastiano Vigna, “Monotone Minimal Perfect Hash Functions,”
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_639, © Springer
Science+Business Media New York, 2016.

No book text, source code, figures, or tables are copied into the ports. The
ports implement the algorithmic description in a small explicit-table profile
and make no claim to reproduce the chapter's succinct encodings or their
space/time bounds. This notice is attribution and scope information; it does
not grant rights to redistribute the book.
