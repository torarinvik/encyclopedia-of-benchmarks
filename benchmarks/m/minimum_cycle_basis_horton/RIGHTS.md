# Rights and provenance

The minimum cycle basis problem and Horton candidate-set algorithm are based
on J. D. Horton, “A Polynomial-Time Algorithm to Find the Shortest Cycle Basis
of a Graph,” *SIAM Journal on Computing* 16(2), 358–366 (1987),
[DOI 10.1137/0216026](https://doi.org/10.1137/0216026). The shortest-path-tree
candidate construction and GF(2) extraction are also described by Mehlhorn and
Michail, [“Minimum Cycle Bases: Faster and Simpler”](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~mehlhorn/ftp/MinCycleBasisFasterSimpler.pdf).

The publications remain copyrighted. This package contains no copied source
code, figures, or extended passages. The bounded contract, candidate handling,
references, workload, and ten language ports are independently authored from
the published algorithmic ideas.
