# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation of the published
permutation-graph minimal-dominating-set counting method. The algorithm is
attributed to Kanté, Limouzy, Mary, Nourine, and Uno, ISAAC 2013, §4,
Propositions 17–19 and Theorem 20,
[DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-45030-3_32](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-45030-3_32).
The bounded scope is also cross-referenced to Encyclopedia of Algorithms
Chapter 721, [DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_721](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_721).

No source code, paper prose, figure, task statement, examples, or tests are
copied. The primary and independent references, fixtures, metadata, and native
language ports were authored for this benchmark. Citations identify algorithm
sources; they do not assert a source-code license.
