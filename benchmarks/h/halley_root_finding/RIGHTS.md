# Rights and provenance

The algorithm reference is NIST's Digital Library of Mathematical Functions,
[§3.8(v), Halley's rule](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.8#E12). It supplies the
mathematical iteration used here. The benchmark specializes the method to the
fixed polynomial `x^3-2`; the bounded contract, ports, fixtures, and reference
implementations were independently authored.

No external code, prose, figures, or workload examples were reused. The source
is cited as mathematical provenance, not as a license to reproduce its text.
