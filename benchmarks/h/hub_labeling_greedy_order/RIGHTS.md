This is a clean-room implementation of the bounded greedy-order and canonical
hierarchical-label procedures described in:

Daniel Delling, Andrew V. Goldberg, and Renato F. Werneck, “Hub Labeling
(2-Hop Labeling),” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI
10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_580, © Springer Science+Business Media New York, 2016.
