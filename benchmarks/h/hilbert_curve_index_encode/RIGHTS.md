# Rights and provenance

The benchmark is based on the algorithmic description in A. R. Butz,
[“Alternative Algorithm for Hilbert's Space-Filling Curve”](https://doi.org/10.1109/T-C.1971.223258),
*IEEE Transactions on Computers* 20(4), 1971, pp. 424–426.

The paper is used only to identify the mathematical procedure. The benchmark
contract, orientation, bounded domain, references, fixtures, documentation,
and all ten language ports are independently authored. No paper code, prose,
examples, problem statements, or tests are reproduced.
