# Provenance

Gurmeet Singh Manku and Rajeev Motwani introduced Lossy Counting in
[*Approximate Frequency Counts over Data Streams*](https://www.vldb.org/conf/2002/S10P03.pdf)
(2002). Only the algorithmic idea is used; code, contract, and fixtures are
independently authored. No upstream source, examples, or prose are copied.
