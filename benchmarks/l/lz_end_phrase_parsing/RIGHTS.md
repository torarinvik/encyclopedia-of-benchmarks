# Rights and provenance

The LZ-End parsing rule is attributed to Sebastian Kreft and Gonzalo Navarro,
“LZ77-like Compression with Fast Random Access,” *2010 Data Compression
Conference*, pp. 239–248, [DOI 10.1109/DCC.2010.29](https://doi.org/10.1109/DCC.2010.29).
The expanded description appears in “On compressing and indexing repetitive
sequences,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 483 (2013), pp. 115–133,
[DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2012.02.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2012.02.006).

This package uses the published algorithmic definition and attribution only.
It does not reproduce source code, examples, figures, or prose. The bounded
contract, scalar summary, primary and independent references, workload,
documentation, and ten language ports are clean-room work. Rights in the
cited publications remain with their authors and publishers.
