# Source and rights

- Algorithm source: Eugene L. Lawler, “Fast Approximation Algorithms for
  Knapsack Problems,” *Mathematics of Operations Research* 4(4), 339–356
  (1979), [DOI 10.1287/moor.4.4.339](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.4.4.339).
- Detailed source: Lawler's 1977 Berkeley technical report,
  [ERL-M77/45](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/1977/Archive/ERL-m-77-45.pdf).
- The benchmark uses the algorithmic ideas of the lower bound, profit
  separation, dyadic scaling, equal-q pruning and grouped dynamic programming.
  The bounded interface, tie rules, output encoding, references, fixtures, and
  ports are original clean-room work. No paper source code is redistributed.
