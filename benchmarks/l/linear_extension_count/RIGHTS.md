# Rights and provenance

This package implements the subset/order-ideal recurrence for counting linear
extensions described in Kustaa Kangas, Teemu Hankala, Teppo Mikael Niinimäki,
and Mikko Koivisto, “Counting Linear Extensions of Sparse Posets,”
*Proceedings of the Twenty-Fifth International Joint Conference on Artificial
Intelligence* (IJCAI 2016),
[official proceedings page](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2016/92) and
[paper PDF](https://www.cs.helsinki.fi/u/mkhkoivi/publications/ijcai-2016.pdf).

The package uses the mathematical algorithm and recurrence only. The language
ports and reference implementations were written specifically for this
bounded benchmark; no third-party implementation, code, examples, or prose was
copied. The publication and linked PDF retain their own rights and terms.
