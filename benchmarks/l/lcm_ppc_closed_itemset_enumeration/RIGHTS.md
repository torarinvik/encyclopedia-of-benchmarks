# Rights and provenance

This package is an independent, clean-room implementation of the LCM
prefix-preserving-closure enumeration procedure. Its method is described in
Takeaki Uno et al., “An Efficient Algorithm for Enumerating Closed Patterns
in Transaction Databases,” Sections 2 and 4.3, DOI
[10.1137/S0097539794275872](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539794275872), and
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, “Frequent Pattern Mining,” Chapter 722, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_722](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_722).

The paper and chapter are used for algorithm attribution and method
specification only. No source code, protected prose, figure, or workload
instance is reproduced. The bounded fixtures and both references were written
independently for this package. The cited publication's copyright and terms
remain with its publisher and authors; attribution here does not grant rights
to redistribute the publication.
