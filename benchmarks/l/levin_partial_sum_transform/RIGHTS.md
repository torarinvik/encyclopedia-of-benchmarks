# Rights and provenance

The mathematical transformation and coefficients follow the algorithm
description in the [NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions,
§3.9(v), Eqs. 3.9.13–3.9.14](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.9#E13). This benchmark is
a clean-room bounded reconstruction. The local contract, exact integer
product-clearing form, references, fixtures, and ports are independently
written.

No source code, comments, prose, example values, or tests have been copied.
The DLMF website's terms govern its text and presentation; no license for
copied material is claimed.
