# Rights and provenance

The historical algorithm idea is attributed to Liu Hui. The mathematical
description and recurrence were consulted in Wolfram MathWorld's
[Liu Hui's Exhaustion Method](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/LiuHuisExhaustionMethod.html)
and cross-checked with the University of St Andrews MacTutor
[Liu Hui biography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Liu_Hui/).
The public Trekhleb JavaScript Algorithms
[index](https://github.com/trekhleb/javascript-algorithms#algorithms) supplied
only the discovery title. No upstream implementation, task statement, example,
test, or prose was copied. The contract, references, fixtures, and language
ports are independently written mathematical formulations.
