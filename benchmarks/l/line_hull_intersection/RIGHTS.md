# Rights and provenance

The line/convex-polygon intersection idea was checked against Bernard
Chazelle and David P. Dobkin, [“Intersection of Convex Objects in Two and
Three Dimensions”](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~chazelle/pubs/IntersectConvexObjects2-3Dim.pdf),
and Kurt Mehlhorn, [“Convex Polygons”](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~mehlhorn/ftp/EATCSmonograph/chapter8.pdf).
The KACTL [geometry chapter index](https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/geometry/chapter.tex)
was used for title discovery; its public
[`LineHullIntersection.h` description](https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/geometry/LineHullIntersection.h)
was consulted to confirm the result pair and degeneracy categories. No
third-party implementation code, tests, examples, or prose were copied. The
bounded contract, references, fixtures, docs, and ports were independently
written for this repository.
