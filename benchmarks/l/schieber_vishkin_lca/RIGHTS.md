# Rights and provenance

The package implements the Schieber–Vishkin lowest-common-ancestor method
from published algorithm descriptions. The references are Baruch Schieber
and Uzi Vishkin, “On Finding Lowest Common Ancestors: Simplification and
Parallelization,” *SIAM Journal on Computing* 17(6), 1253–1262 (1988),
[DOI 10.1137/0217079](https://doi.org/10.1137/0217079), and the operational
description in Volker Heun, *Algorithmische Bioinformatik: Bäume und
Graphen*, section 4.2, [lecture notes](https://www.bio.ifi.lmu.de/mitarbeiter/volker-heun/notes/as9.pdf).
Schieber's related 1994 chapter is listed on the
[author's publication page](https://sbarnjit.github.io/).

The implementations, workload, and references in this package are
independently written. No source code, task text, worked example, test data,
or fixture was copied from RosettaCodeData or from the method references.
RosettaCodeData was consulted only for its pinned title and visible popularity
score. This notice does not assert a license for the referenced publications.
