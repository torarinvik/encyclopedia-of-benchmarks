# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Kida, Takeda, Shinohara, and Arikawa,
“Shift-And Approach to Pattern Matching in LZW Compressed Text,” CPM 1999,
and to the related *Encyclopedia of Algorithms* chapter on pattern matching
on compressed text. The paper is cited for algorithm ideas only. No paper
source code, examples, or prose are reproduced. The benchmark contract,
references, fixtures, and language ports are independently authored.
