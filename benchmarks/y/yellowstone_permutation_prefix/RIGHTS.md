# Rights and provenance

The benchmark implements the mathematical recurrence for the Yellowstone permutation described by Applegate, Havermann, Selcoe, Shevelev, Sloane, and Zumkeller, “The Yellowstone Permutation,” *Journal of Integer Sequences* 18 (2015), Article 15.6.7: [paper](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/JIS/VOL18/Sloane/sloane9.pdf). [OEIS A098550](https://oeis.org/A098550) provides a sequence cross-reference. The RosettaCodeData [popular-tasks title index](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/main/popular-tasks) served only as discovery evidence.

Only the mathematical definition is used. No Rosetta task text, code, examples, tests, fixtures, or prose were copied. The bounded contract, workload, language ports, and independent references are original to this repository.
