# Rights and provenance

The algorithm profile is based on the mathematics of Vogel's approximation
method as discussed in Harvey H. Shore, “The Transportation Problem and the
Vogel Approximation Method,” *Decision Sciences* 1 (1970), 441–457,
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5915.1970.tb00792.x. The row/column penalty and
allocation steps are cross-checked against the open Open University chapter
https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/28668/1/Unit-7.pdf. The RosettaGit
task-title page https://rosettagit.org/tasks/vogels-approximation-method/ was
used as a discovery index only; no task text, examples, or code were reused.

Every implementation, oracle, fixture, and explanation was written
independently for this repository. The profile adds deterministic bounded tie
rules and does not redistribute third-party code or prose.
