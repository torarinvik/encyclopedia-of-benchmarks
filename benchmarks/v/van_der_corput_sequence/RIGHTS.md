# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is the mathematical radical-inverse definition: expand each
positive index in base `b`, reflect the digits around the radix point, and
interpret the reversed digits as a fraction. The definition is documented by
John Burkardt's University of South Carolina
[Van der Corput reference](https://people.math.sc.edu/Burkardt/f_src/van_der_corput/van_der_corput.html).

The RosettaGit [public task-title index](https://rosettagit.org/tasks/) supplied
the discovery title “Van der Corput sequence”; its footer identifies the mirror
content as GFDL-1.3-or-later. The index was used as a title-only algorithm
catalog. No Rosetta task prose, examples, test cases, submitted implementations,
or code were copied. The ten language ports and both mathematical references
were written independently for this repository.
