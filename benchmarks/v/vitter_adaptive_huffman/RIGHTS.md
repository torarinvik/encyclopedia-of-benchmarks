# Rights and source use

This is a clean-room bounded benchmark of Vitter's Algorithm A for adaptive
Huffman coding. NIST DADS and the cited Vitter and Knuth publications are used
for algorithm identification and method descriptions only. No upstream code,
tests, figures, examples, or prose are included. The input profile, NYT/literal
framing, deterministic bit packing, workloads, references, and ports were
written independently. No upstream source-code license is asserted.
