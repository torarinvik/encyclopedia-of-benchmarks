# Rights and provenance

The algorithm idea and formulas were cross-checked with Francis E. Greulich,
[“Accurate Polygon Centroid Computation Using ARC/INFO GIS”](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)0887-3801(1995)9:1(88)),
and its [author-hosted copy](https://faculty.washington.edu/greulich/Documents/Centroid.pdf),
as well as the University of Melbourne's
[computational geometry notes](https://tomkom.pages.gitlab.unimelb.edu.au/spatialdatamanagement/21_geometrical_analysis.html).
The public KACTL
[geometry chapter index](https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/geometry/chapter.tex)
provided the discovery title `PolygonCenter.h`; its public
[header description](https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/geometry/PolygonCenter.h)
was consulted to confirm the operation. No KACTL source code, prose, examples,
or tests were copied. The bounded contract, references,
fixtures, documentation, and language ports were independently written for
this repository.
