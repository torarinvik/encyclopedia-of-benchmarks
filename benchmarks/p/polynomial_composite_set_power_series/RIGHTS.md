# Rights and provenance

The mathematical operation, dense mask coefficient ordering, and modulus are
based on the [Library Checker task](https://github.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/blob/master/set_power_series/polynomial_composite_set_power_series/task.md)
and its [official parameter file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/master/set_power_series/polynomial_composite_set_power_series/info.toml).
The upstream repository is licensed under Apache-2.0; see its
[license](https://github.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/blob/master/LICENSE).
The bounded profile, workload, ports, oracles, and documentation here are
clean-room implementations of the operation. No upstream code, tests, fixture
values, or prose were reused.
