# Rights and provenance

The signed face-tetrahedron volume identity was checked against Keenan
Crane's [piecewise-planar mesh volume formula](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/Other/QuadMeshVolume.pdf)
and Brian Mirtich's [polyhedral mass-properties paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/10867651.1996.10487458).
KACTL's [geometry chapter index](https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/geometry/chapter.tex)
was used for title discovery; the public
[`PolyhedronVolume.h` description](https://github.com/kth-competitive-programming/kactl/blob/main/content/geometry/PolyhedronVolume.h)
was consulted to confirm that the input is an already supplied oriented
triangle mesh. No third-party code, tests, examples, or prose were copied.
The bounded contract, references, fixtures, documentation, and ten language
ports were independently written for this repository.
