# Rights and provenance

The ten implementations, workload, and documentation are original clean-room
work based on the mathematical definition and unique square/cube
representation. The pinned RosettaCodeData popular-task index is cited only as
the source of the title discovery; no task wording, submitted code, examples,
or tests are reused.

Method context is the unique representation of a powerful number as
`a^2*b^3` with squarefree `b`, stated in Chan, “Arithmetic progressions among
powerful numbers,” *Journal of Integer Sequences* 26 (2023),
[article PDF](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL26/Chan/chan33.pdf).
The definition and sequence identification are in OEIS
[A001694](https://oeis.org/A001694). No third-party implementation is included.

No program, helper, checker, script, test, build, compiler, profiler, or
benchmark was run to create or validate these files.
