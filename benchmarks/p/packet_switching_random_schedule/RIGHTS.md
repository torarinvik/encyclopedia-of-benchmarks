# Rights and provenance

The policy is attributed to Susanne Albers and Tobias Jacobs, “An Experimental
Study of New and Known Online Packet Buffering Algorithms,” Algorithmica
57(4), 725–746, DOI
[10.1007/s00453-008-9230-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-008-9230-y), §3.2.
The application context is Markus Schmidt, “Packet Switching in Multi-queue
Switches,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI
[10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_274](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_274).

These publications are cited for algorithm provenance, not as code licenses.
The package was independently written from the algorithm description. No
published source code, comments, prose, problem text, figures, examples, or
fixtures were reused. Caller-supplied priority words make the trace
deterministic; the profile makes no randomized-performance guarantee.
