# Rights and provenance

The algorithm profile is based on the description of point location in
Marcel Roeloffzen, “Point Location,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, chapter
587, DOI [10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_587](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_587),
and on the stochastic visibility-walk discussion in Olivier Devillers,
Sylvain Pion, and Monique Teillaud, “Walking in a Triangulation,”
*International Journal of Foundations of Computer Science* 13 (2002),
181–199, DOI [10.1142/S0129054102001047](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129054102001047).

The cited chapter and paper remain under their publishers' copyrights. This
repository uses their algorithm descriptions only. All ten implementations,
oracles, workload fixtures, and metadata were written independently; no source
code or book prose was copied. The bounded contract supplies its own exact
integer geometry limits, local edge order, binary branch tape, and stopping
statuses.
