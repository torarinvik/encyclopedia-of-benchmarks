# Rights and provenance

The source is Haverkort and Toma, “Quadtrees and Morton Indexing,” Chapter
585 of the *Encyclopedia of Algorithms* (2016),
[doi:10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_585](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_585).
The PMR section describes incremental segment insertion, threshold-triggered
four-way subdivision, and redistribution to intersected regions.

Only the mathematical algorithm description was consulted. The input bounds,
exact integer geometry, closed-cell split convention, depth and node caps,
binary serialization, workload, and implementations are independently
written. No source code, prose, figures, or examples were reproduced. Rights
in the cited work remain with its authors and publisher.
