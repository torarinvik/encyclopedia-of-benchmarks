# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Stephen C. Pohlig and Martin E. Hellman,
“An improved algorithm for computing logarithms over GF(p) and its
cryptographic significance,” *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 24(1)
(1978), 106–110. The [author-hosted paper](https://ee.stanford.edu/~hellman/publications/28.pdf)
and [FLINT's algorithm documentation](https://flintlib.org/doc/algorithms/number-theory/discrete-log.html)
were consulted for mathematical algorithm ideas.

No source code, source prose, examples, or tests were copied. This package's
contract, ports, direct-power reference, workload, and documentation were
independently authored.
