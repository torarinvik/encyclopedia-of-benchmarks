# Rights and provenance

Algorithm source: NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, §3.11(iv), “Padé Approximations,” Eqs. 3.11.20–3.11.23: https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.11.iv . These equations define a Padé approximant as a numerator and denominator whose product relation matches a formal series prefix.

The DLMF was consulted for mathematical behavior only. This package does not reproduce source code, comments, example coefficients, tests, or prose. The modular field, degree bounds, input/output ABI, singular-system status, fixtures, and language ports were independently specified and written for this benchmark.
