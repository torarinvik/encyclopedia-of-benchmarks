# Rights and provenance

The theorem is attributed to H. C. Pocklington, “The determination of the
prime or composite nature of large numbers by Fermat's theorem,” *Proceedings
of the Cambridge Philosophical Society* 18 (1914), 29–30. A scan is available
in the [Cambridge Philosophical Society proceedings archive](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/eb/Proceedings_-_Cambridge_Philosophical_Society_%28IA_proceedingscambr18camb%29.pdf).
The modern statement is cross-referenced in [FLINT's primality-proving
documentation](https://flintlib.org/doc/algorithms/number-theory/primality-proving.html).

This package uses the theorem as a mathematical idea only. Its contract,
factor and witness fixtures, source ports, primary/reference oracles, and prose
were independently authored; no upstream implementation or examples were
copied.
