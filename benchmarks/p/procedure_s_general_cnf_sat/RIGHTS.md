# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation of the Procedure S recurrence described in “Exact Algorithms for General CNF SAT,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_133`.

The chapter and summarized research remain copyrighted. This package reproduces no chapter prose, source code, or figures and asserts no license over the source material. Its exhaustive truth-table subroutine, deterministic tie order, bounds, and trace output are benchmark-profile refinements; the implementation makes no claim to the chapter's running-time bound.
