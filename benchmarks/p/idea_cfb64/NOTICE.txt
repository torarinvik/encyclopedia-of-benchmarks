IDEA was developed by Xuejia Lai and James L. Massey of ETH Zurich. The PGP source implementations in cBench/security_pgp_d and cBench/security_pgp_e attribute modifications and extensive optimizations to Colin Plumb. Their pinned idea.c files provide the encrypt and decrypt CFB helpers represented by this combined profile.
Both pinned sources state that no license fee is required for noncommercial use and direct commercial users to contact Ascom-Tech AG regarding licensing. They identify patents PCT/CH91/00117, EP 0 482 154 B1, and US005214703 and warn that commercial use may require licensing. Neither pinned source states an explicit source-code redistribution license.
This package is an independent implementation of IDEA CFB64 encryption and decryption and contains no copied upstream code or comments. This notice records provenance and caveats; it does not resolve source redistribution or patent questions.
