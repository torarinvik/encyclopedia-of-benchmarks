# Source and rights

- Algorithm source: Esko Ukkonen, “On-line Construction of Suffix Trees,”
  *Algorithmica* 14 (1995), 249–260,
  [DOI 10.1007/BF01206331](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01206331).
- The benchmark uses the algorithmic ideas of online phases, active points,
  suffix links, open leaf ends, canonization, and edge splitting. Its bounded
  contract, canonical serialization, references, fixtures, and language ports
  are clean-room work. No source code is redistributed.
