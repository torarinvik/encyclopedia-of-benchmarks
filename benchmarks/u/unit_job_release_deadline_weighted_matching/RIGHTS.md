# Provenance and reuse

The matching formulation and greedy feasibility idea were consulted in
[Dekel and Sahni's paper on convex bipartite matching and scheduling](https://www.cise.ufl.edu/~sahni/papers/convexBipartite.pdf).
The paper is used for mathematical ideas, not copied source or prose. The
implementations, bounded contract, reference, and cases are independently
authored for this repository; no permission to reuse the paper's text is assumed.
