enumeration. Its circular member-list behavior is based on the algorithm
description and public reference for
NyaanNyaan/library's
[`union-find-enumerate.hpp`](https://github.com/NyaanNyaan/library/blob/master/data-structure/union-find-enumerate.hpp).
The upstream repository declares CC0 1.0 Universal in its
[`LICENSE`](https://github.com/NyaanNyaan/library/blob/master/LICENSE).
No upstream code, prose, tests, or fixtures are included. The implementations,
oracles, and workload are original repository material and follow the
repository's license.
