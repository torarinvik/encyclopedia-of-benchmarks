# Rights and provenance

The mathematical method is based on Stuart J. Berkowitz, “On computing the
determinant in small parallel time using a small number of processors,”
*Information Processing Letters* 18(3), 147–150 (1984), DOI
<https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(84)90018-8>. The publisher's record is at
<https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0020019084900188>; a paper
copy is available at
<https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~r5olivei/courses/2021-winter-cs487/berkowitz-paper.pdf>.

Only the division-free mathematical recurrence is used. The bounded modular
contract, fixtures, oracle implementations, language ports, and prose were
created independently. No paper code, prose, figures, or examples are
reproduced.
