# Rights and provenance

The operation contract, zero-constant condition, and modulus are based on the Apache-2.0 [Library Checker composition task](https://github.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/blob/master/polynomial/composition_of_formal_power_series_large/task.md) and its [base variant](https://github.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/blob/master/polynomial/composition_of_formal_power_series/task.md). The algorithm description is based on Brent and Kung, “Fast Algorithms for Manipulating Formal Power Series,” *Journal of the ACM* 25(4), 1978, [DOI 10.1145/322092.322099](https://doi.org/10.1145/322092.322099), with a modern explanatory cross-reference to [FLINT's composition documentation](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/blob/main/doc/src/algorithms/polynomials/composition.md).

Only mathematical definitions and algorithm structure are used. No task solution, library implementation, or source prose was copied. The ten ports, workload, fixtures, and oracles are clean-room work written for this repository.
