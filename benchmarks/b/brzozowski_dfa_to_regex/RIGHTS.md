# Rights and provenance

The pinned RosettaCodeData popular-task index is cited only as the source of
the discovery title. Its task implementations and prose were not opened,
copied, or used as implementation material.

The mathematical method follows the algebraic system of regular-language
equations associated with Brzozowski and Arden elimination. The method sources
are cited in [README.md](README.md): Brzozowski and Leiss, “On Equations for
Regular Languages, Finite Automata, and Sequential Networks,” and Brzozowski,
“Derivatives of Regular Expressions.” These publications are used for
mathematical ideas only. No source code, worked examples, figures, tests, or
prose were copied. The contract, canonical serializer, references, fixtures,
inventory, and ten native implementations are independently authored.

The cited academic publications are not treated as code licenses; this package
does not distribute their text or code. The Rust reference's derivative-based
equivalence check is separately authored from the published definition of
regular-expression derivatives.
