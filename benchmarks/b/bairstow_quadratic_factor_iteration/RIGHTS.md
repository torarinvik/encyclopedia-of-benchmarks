# Rights and provenance

The mathematical procedure is from NIST DLMF [§3.8(iv)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.8.iv),
especially [Eqs. 3.8.10–3.8.11](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.8.iv#E10). DLMF
identifies Bairstow's method as a procedure for polynomial zeros with real
coefficients. The finite-field trace is an explicit benchmark adaptation of
the algebraic recurrence and does not claim the original real convergence
result. No DLMF source code, comments, prose, examples, or tests are copied.
The contract, references, fixtures, and native ports are clean-room work.
