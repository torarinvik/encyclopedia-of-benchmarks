# Rights and provenance

The benchmark uses the Berlekamp–Welch decoding idea described by Welch and
Berlekamp in [U.S. Patent 4,633,470](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4633470A/en)
and the evaluation-form presentation in Appendix A.1 of Trevisan's
[coding-theory survey hosted by Stanford](https://theory.stanford.edu/~trevisan/pubs/codingsurvey.pdf).
The patent is expired. No source code, examples, problem text, or test data were
copied. The finite-field contract, Gaussian-elimination decoder, exhaustive
oracle, fixtures, and language ports are independently authored.
