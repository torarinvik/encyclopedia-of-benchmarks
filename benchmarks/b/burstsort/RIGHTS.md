# Source and rights

- Algorithm source: Ranjan Sinha and Justin Zobel, “Cache-Conscious Sorting
  of Large Sets of Strings with Dynamic Tries,” ACM Journal of Experimental
  Algorithmics 9 (2004), DOI [10.1145/1005813.1041517](https://doi.org/10.1145/1005813.1041517).
- The benchmark uses the paper's algorithmic ideas only: trie insertion,
  capacity-triggered bucket bursting, and lexicographic traversal. The bounded
  input format, threshold, result encoding, references, and language ports are
  original clean-room work. No paper source code is redistributed.
