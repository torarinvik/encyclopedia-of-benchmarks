# Rights and provenance

This package implements a bounded fixed-radix specialization of Barrett's
reciprocal-based modular reduction, described in Paul Barrett, “Implementing
the Rivest Shamir and Adleman Public Key Encryption Algorithm on a Standard
Digital Signal Processor,” *Advances in Cryptology — CRYPTO ’86*, Lecture
Notes in Computer Science 263, 311–323 (1987). See the cited
[paper copy](https://www.loper-os.org/pub/barrett_1986.pdf) and
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-47721-7_24).

Only the mathematical algorithm and its bounded contract are used. The ports,
oracles, fixtures, and documentation were written for this benchmark; no
published code or text was copied. The publication and paper copy retain their
own rights and terms.
