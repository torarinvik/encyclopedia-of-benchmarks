# Provenance

The factor-oracle and backward-search ideas are attributed to Cyril Allauzen,
Maxime Crochemore, and Mathieu Raffinot, [*Factor Oracle: A New Structure for
Pattern Matching*](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=4799c88ba0bd0626f9e4916ef3f530e2c8e677bf&repid=rep1&type=pdf)
(1999). The contract, code, and reference drafts are independently authored;
no upstream code, tests, or prose are copied.
