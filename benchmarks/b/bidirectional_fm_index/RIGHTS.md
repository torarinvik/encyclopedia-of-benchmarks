# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic method is attributed to T. W. Lam, Ruiqiang Li, Alan Tam, Simon Wong, Edward Wu, and S. M. Yiu, “High Throughput Short Read Alignment via Bi-directional BWT,” 2009 IEEE International Conference on Bioinformatics and Biomedicine, pp. 31–36, DOI [10.1109/BIBM.2009.42](https://doi.org/10.1109/BIBM.2009.42). The authors' [institutional record and paper](https://hub.hku.hk/handle/10722/129577) identify the publication and its IEEE copyright.

This package uses only the published algorithmic idea of synchronized forward and reverse suffix-array intervals with BWT rank updates. Its bounded alphabet, input contract, checksum, fixtures, reference implementations, and language ports are independently authored. No paper code, prose, figures, examples, or experimental data are reproduced. The profile does not claim to reproduce the full read-alignment application or its compressed-space representation.

This note records provenance; it is not legal advice.
