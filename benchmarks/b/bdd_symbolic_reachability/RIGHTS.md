# Rights and provenance

This benchmark is a clean-room bounded profile of symbolic reachability using
ROBDDs. The algorithm context is Aziz and Prakash, “Symbolic Model Checking,”
[*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 416](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_416),
with the reduced ordered BDD representation cross-referenced to their
[Binary Decision Graph chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_48).

The cited material supplies algorithm ideas and bibliographic provenance only.
The finite transition-table encoding, invariant result, workload, independent
explicit-state reference, and all language ports were written for this
benchmark. No book prose, source code, figures, examples, or tests were copied.
