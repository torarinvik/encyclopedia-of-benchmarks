# Rights and provenance

The finite-state-machine-minimization problem was identified in NIST DADS,
[“finite state machine minimization”](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/finitestmmin.html).
The implemented method is Brzozowski's reverse-and-determinize construction,
from “Canonical Regular Expressions and Minimal State Graphs for Definite
Events,” *Mathematical Theory of Automata* 12 (1962), 529–561. The theorem is
also stated in the [Prague Stringology proceedings](https://psc.fit.cvut.cz/cgi-bin/getfile.cgi?c=-&n=11&t=pdf&y=2002).

These sources were used for algorithm identification and mathematical ideas
only. This package's bounded contract, source files, fixtures, references, and
documentation are original. No upstream code, prose, examples, or figures are
included.
