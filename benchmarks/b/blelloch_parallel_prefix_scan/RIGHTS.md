# Rights and provenance

The scan definition and the work-efficient parallel tree procedure are based
on Guy E. Blelloch, *Prefix Sums and Their Applications*, Carnegie Mellon
University School of Computer Science Technical Report CMU-CS-90-190,
November 1990, Section 1.2. See the [CMU report record](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/project/scandal/public/papers/CMU-CS-90-190.html)
and [report text](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~guyb/papers/Ble93.pdf).

Only the algorithmic definition and mathematical method are used. The bounded
contract, source files, fixtures, independent reference, and documentation
were authored for this benchmark. No upstream code, prose, or examples are
included.
