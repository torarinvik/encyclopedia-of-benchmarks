# Rights and provenance

The mathematical method is from NIST DLMF [§3.3(i), Eq. 3.3.1](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.3.i#E1)
and its weight definition [Eq. 3.3.3_1](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.3.i#E3_1).

examples, or prose have been copied. This benchmark's bounded finite-field
contract, fixtures, references, and language ports are clean-room work.
