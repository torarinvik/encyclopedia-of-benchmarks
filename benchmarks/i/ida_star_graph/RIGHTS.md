# Rights and provenance

The IDA* method is attributed to Richard E. Korf, [“Iterative-Deepening-A*:
An Optimal Admissible Tree Search” (IJCAI 1985)](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1985/208).
The package's bounded graph contract, implementations, and BFS reference were
independently written from the method description. No paper code, prose, or
examples are reproduced, and no license for the paper is implied.
