# Rights and provenance

The operational attribution is to Erik D. Demaine, Alejandro López-Ortiz, and
J. Ian Munro, “Experiments on Adaptive Set Intersections for Text Retrieval
Systems,” §3, Proceedings of the 3rd Workshop on Algorithm Engineering and
Experiments (ALENEX 2001), DOI 10.1007/3-540-44808-X_7. The underlying
intersection framework is from their “Adaptive Set Intersections, Unions, and
Differences,” SODA 2000, pp. 743–752. Chapter context is Andrew Kane and Alejandro López-Ortiz,
“Intersections of Inverted Lists,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI
10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_628.
