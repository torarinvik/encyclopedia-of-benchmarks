# Rights and provenance

“Shortest Knight Path (Infinite Chessboard)” was used as a public title for
candidate discovery only. The mathematical method follows the functional
distance formula of P. P. Das and B. N. Chatterji, “Knight's distance in digital
geometry,” *Pattern Recognition Letters* 7(4), 1988, pp. 215–226,
[doi:10.1016/0167-8655(88)90105-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-8655(88)90105-5).

The bounded scalar contract, direct formula implementation, small-displacement
BFS reference, workload, documentation, and ten ports are clean-room work. No
Codewars statement, examples, tests, solutions, or submissions were read or
copied.
