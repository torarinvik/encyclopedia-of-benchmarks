# Rights and provenance

The algorithm profile is attributed to Zaixin Lu and Weili Wu, “Influence
Maximization,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 710, pp. 985–987,
[DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_710](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_710),
especially the random Linear Threshold model and the greedy algorithm in Fig.
1. The book's chapter is copyrighted by Springer; no source-code license is
asserted for it.

The random-LT triggering-set interpretation is attributed to David Kempe,
Jon Kleinberg, and Éva Tardos, “Maximizing the Spread of Influence through a
Social Network,” Section 4.3, Theorem 4.6,
[DOI 10.4086/toc.2015.v011a004](https://doi.org/10.4086/toc.2015.v011a004). The
open article identifies its license as CC BY. This benchmark uses its
mathematical construction only.

No published code, pseudocode, test fixtures, comments, or article prose were
copied. The ten ports and both references are clean-room implementations of a
bounded discrete profile. The supplied draw tape is deterministic; no claim
is made about the probabilistic accuracy of a finite sample.
