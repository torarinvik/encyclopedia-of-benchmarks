# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Juha Kärkkäinen and Esko Ukkonen's
“Two- and Higher-Dimensional Pattern Matching in Optimal Expected Time,”
especially Sections 4 and 6.2 and Lemma 6.1, and to their Encyclopedia of
Algorithms chapter “Multidimensional String Matching.” These sources are used
for algorithm provenance and attribution.

This package independently implements a bounded four-symbol,
Hamming-distance-at-most-one profile. It does not include upstream source
code, copied problem text, protected prose, or third-party test data. The
language ports and fixtures were authored for this repository.
