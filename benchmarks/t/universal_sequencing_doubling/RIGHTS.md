# Rights and attribution

This package is an independently written implementation of the deterministic Doubling Algorithm described by Julián Mestre and Nicole Megow in “Universal Sequencing on an Unreliable Machine,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_543`.

The chapter, its figure, and the cited original research remain copyrighted. This package asserts no license to redistribute those materials and includes no copied code, prose, or figure. Its nine ports implement only the mathematical knapsack-selection and reverse-level sequence construction with explicit deterministic tie rules. Preserve the bibliographic attribution above when redistributing this package.
