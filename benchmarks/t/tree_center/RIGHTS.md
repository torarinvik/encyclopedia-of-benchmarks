# Rights and provenance

The benchmark takes the tree-center algorithm idea from William Fiset's
`TreeCenter.java` in the MIT-licensed
[Algorithms repository](https://github.com/williamfiset/Algorithms). No
source code, comments, prose, tests, or fixtures were copied. The bounded
contract, ten ports, workload, and both references are independently
authored. The implementation uses the standard leaf-layer stop condition:
retain the last one or two active vertices.
