# Rights and provenance

Algorithm attribution: Jérémy Barbay and Claire Kenyon, “Adaptive Intersection
and t-Threshold Problems,” Section 4.2, Algorithm 3, DOI
10.1145/545381.545432. Chapter context: Andrew Kane and Alejandro
López-Ortiz, “Intersections of Inverted Lists,” Encyclopedia of Algorithms,
DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_628.

The paper and chapter are used for attribution and algorithm provenance. This
package contains a new bounded specialization for three nonempty sorted
32-bit integer lists and threshold two. Its ports and fixtures were written
for this repository; no upstream source, copied prose, or third-party test
data is included.
