# Source and rights

Source: Dominik Schultes, “Routing in Road Networks with Transit Nodes,”
Encyclopedia of Algorithms, DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_353
(`OEBPS/AChap353.html`). The chapter supplies the transit-node distance
formula and exactness condition. No source-code license grant is asserted.
The ports are clean-room implementations. The bounded table framing, explicit
far-query flag, integer bounds, and witness tie rule are profile refinements.
