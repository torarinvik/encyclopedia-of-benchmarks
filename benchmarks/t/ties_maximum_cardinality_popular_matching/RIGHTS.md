# Rights and provenance

The algorithm description is based on Abraham, Irving, Kavitha, and
Mehlhorn, “Popular Matchings,” *SIAM Journal on Computing* 37(4),
<https://doi.org/10.1137/06067328X>, and its author-hosted version at
<https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/3494/1/abraham3494.pdf>. The chapter crosswalk is
Kavitha Telikepalli, “Ranked Matching,”
<https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_331>.

The University of Glasgow record states that the author version is posted by
permission of ACM for personal use and not for redistribution. No paper or
chapter files are redistributed here. The bounded input/output contract,
fixtures, direct-vote reference, alternating-graph reference, and ten ports
are clean-room implementations based only on the mathematical definitions
and algorithm descriptions. No source code, examples, figures, or prose have
been reused, and no source-code license is asserted for the publications.
