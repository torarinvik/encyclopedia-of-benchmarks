# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic source is Berthold Vöcking, “How Asymmetry Helps Load
Balancing,” *Journal of the ACM* 50(4), 568–589 (2003),
[DOI 10.1145/792538.792546](https://doi.org/10.1145/792538.792546). The
[NIST DADS 2-left hashing entry](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/twoLeftHashing.html)
provides the concrete two-table placement rule and points to Vöcking's work.

Only algorithmic ideas are used. No source code, prose, examples, figures, or
fixtures are copied or adapted. Supplied bucket-pair framing, deterministic
table bounds, output fields, references, workload, and ten language ports are
independently authored. Because hash indices are supplied by the caller, this
profile does not claim the probabilistic bounds from the paper.
