TSVC source attribution and rights note

The behavior reference is the TSVC adaptation in the LAC-DCC Benchmarks
repository, commit 5d7b75df749241efa7a6e6f5be613d3fcc5cce38. Its source says
that the non-four-digit codelets are taken from the Test Suite for Vectorizing
Compilers by David Callahan, Jack Dongarra, and David Levine. The pinned TSVC
directory and repository material reviewed for this package did not provide an
explicit license grant for the s331 codelet.

These nine ports and the oracle are clean-room implementations of the behavior
described in the package README. They do not copy or translate upstream source
code or comments, and they do not claim a license to upstream TSVC material.
The attribution is included to identify the source of the algorithmic test
case, not to imply endorsement.
