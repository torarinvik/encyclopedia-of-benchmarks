# Rights and provenance

The profile is based on the mathematical result in Thomassen, “Convex Graph
Drawing,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 652,
[DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_652](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_652),
Theorem 1. The theorem's stated conditions are represented as a bounded
decision problem for a supplied plane embedding and outer-side partition.

All ten ports, the bounded contract, fixtures, package documentation, and both
