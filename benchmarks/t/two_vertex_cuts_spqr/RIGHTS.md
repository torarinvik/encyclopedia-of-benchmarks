# Source and rights

The [Library Checker proposal](https://github.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/issues/1427)
is used for the operation and weighted aggregate contract. Gutwenger and
Mutzel's [SPQR-tree paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-44541-2_8) and the
Hopcroft–Tarjan [triconnected decomposition paper](https://doi.org/10.1137/0202012)
are used as mathematical algorithm references. No source code, implementation
text, examples, or fixtures were copied. The bounded split decomposition,
oracles, contract, and fixtures were written independently.
