# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Juha Kärkkäinen and Esko Ukkonen's static-grid
method in “Two- and Higher-Dimensional Pattern Matching in Optimal Expected
Time” and their Encyclopedia of Algorithms chapter “Multidimensional String
Matching.” The references above describe the method; this package independently
implements a bounded four-symbol workload and does not copy source code,
problem text, tests, or protected prose.

The implementations are new clean-room ports. No upstream implementation or
third-party test corpus is included. The cited algorithm paper and chapter are
used for attribution and algorithm provenance only.
