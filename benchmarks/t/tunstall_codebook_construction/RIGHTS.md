# Rights and provenance

The Tunstall algorithm is attributed to Brian P. Tunstall, *Synthesis of
Noiseless Compression Codes*, Ph.D. thesis, Georgia Institute of Technology.
The construction and expected phrase-length objective are described in the MIT
6.441 [supplementary notes on Tunstall codes](https://web.mit.edu/6.441/spring06/handout/sup/Tunstall_codes_-_Notes_1.pdf),
which cite the thesis.

This package uses the published algorithmic idea and attribution only. It does
not copy source code, examples, figures, or prose. The bounded integer-weight
contract, deterministic tie rule, scalar summary, references, workload,
documentation, and ten language ports are independently authored. Rights in
the cited material remain with its authors and institutions.
