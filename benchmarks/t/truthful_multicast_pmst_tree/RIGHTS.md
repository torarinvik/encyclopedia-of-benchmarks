# Rights and provenance

This package clean-room implements the PMST construction described in Weizhao Wang, Xiang-Yang Li, and Yu Wang, “Truthful Multicast,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_437`.

The chapter and cited research are copyrighted. This package reproduces no source code, prose, or figures and asserts no license over the source material. Its coverage is limited to the MST-root-and-prune tree construction, not the associated payment mechanism.
