# Rights and provenance

Turbo Reverse Factor is described in Chapter 26 of the ESMAJ exact string
matching reference and in the cited paper:

- [ESMAJ algorithm page](https://www-igm.univ-mlv.fr/~lecroq/string/node24.html)
- [ESMAJ reference PDF](https://ligm.univ-eiffel.fr/~lecroq/string/string.pdf)
- Maxime Crochemore, Artur Czumaj, Leszek Gąsieniec, Stefan Jarominek,
  Thierry Lecroq, Wojciech Plandowski, and Wojciech Rytter, “Speeding up two
  string-matching algorithms,” *Algorithmica* 12(4/5), 247–267 (1994),
  [doi:10.1007/BF01185427](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01185427).
- Crochemore and Rytter, *Text Algorithms*, Oxford University Press, 1994,
  also cited by Chapter 26.

Only algorithmic descriptions were consulted. The implementation, bounded
contract, references, workload, and prose are independently written. No
upstream source code, prose, figures, or examples are reproduced. Rights in
the cited works remain with their authors and publishers.
