# Rights and provenance

This benchmark independently implements the DSATUR greedy coloring algorithm
described by Daniel Brélaz in “New Methods to Color the Vertices of a Graph,”
*Communications of the ACM* 22(4), 251–256 (1979),
[doi:10.1145/359094.359101](https://doi.org/10.1145/359094.359101).

The article is an algorithmic reference only. No source code, benchmark code,
fixtures, or prose has been copied. The bounded contract, workload, references,
and language ports are independently authored.
