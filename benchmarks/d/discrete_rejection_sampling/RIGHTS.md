# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is based on the general accept/reject construction described in
Luc Devroye, *Non-Uniform Random Variate Generation*, Chapter II, section 3
(1986), DOI [10.1007/978-1-4613-8643-8_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4613-8643-8_2),
and the Algorithm Archive's rejection-sampling overview at
<https://www.algorithm-archive.org/contents/box_muller/box_muller_rejection.html>.
The Archive licenses its text under CC BY-SA 4.0. Devroye's personal site
provides his book as a readable reference. This repository uses these sources
to identify and understand the algorithm; it does not reproduce their prose,
figures, code, examples, or test data. The bounded deterministic profile,
oracles, workloads, documentation, and ten ports are independently written.
