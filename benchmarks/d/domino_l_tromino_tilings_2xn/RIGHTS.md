# Rights and provenance

The package is a clean-room implementation of a finite tiling count. The
Codewars kata title was consulted only to identify the subject; its statement,
examples, tests, submissions, and code were not read or copied.

Method sources:

- Gregory Dresden and Michael Tulskikh, “Tiling a 2×n Board with Dominos and
  L-Shaped Trominos,” *Journal of Integer Sequences* 24 (2021), Article
  21.4.5, [paper](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL24/Dresden2/dresden9.pdf).
  The paper defines the tile set and derives the scalar recurrence used by the
  independent reference.
- OEIS Foundation, [A052980](https://oeis.org/A052980), including its
  published sequence table; used to cross-check workload values.
- Codewars, [title index entry](https://www.codewars.com/kata/6ac3dbb358ef7bac7b16dd52),
  used only for discovery.

The recurrence and finite-board tiling counts are mathematical results. The
frontier transition table, references, workload, and all ten ports are written
independently. Copyright in cited publications remains with their authors and
publishers.
