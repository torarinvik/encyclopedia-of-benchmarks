# Rights and provenance

This package is an independently authored, bounded implementation of the
Dulmage–Mendelsohn decomposition of bipartite graphs. Algorithm descriptions
are attributed to OI Wiki's [bipartite matching article](https://github.com/OI-wiki/OI-wiki/blob/master/docs/graph/graph-matching/bigraph-match.md)
and to A. L. Dulmage and N. S. Mendelsohn, “Coverings of Bipartite Graphs,”
*Canadian Journal of Mathematics* 10 (1958), 517–534,
[DOI 10.4153/CJM-1958-052-0](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1958-052-0).

No source code, prose, figures, or benchmark instances were copied. The
contract, workload, references, and ports were written independently. OI Wiki
