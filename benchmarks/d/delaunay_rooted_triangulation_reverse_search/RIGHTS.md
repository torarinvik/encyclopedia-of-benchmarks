# Rights and provenance

The source method is attributed to Chapter 729 of the *Encyclopedia of
Algorithms*, DOI [10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_729](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_729),
which cites Avis and Fukuda (1996), “Reverse search for enumeration,”
*Discrete Applied Mathematics* 65(1–3):21–46.

The Lawson-edge parent rule and reverse-search idea are used as mathematical
method references. The repository-defined convex-polygon contract, exact
geometry checks, traversal, independent subset reference, fixtures, and
language ports are clean-room. No chapter or paper code or prose is copied.
