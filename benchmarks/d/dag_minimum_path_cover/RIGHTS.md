# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room bounded implementation of the minimum
vertex-disjoint path-cover reduction for directed acyclic graphs. The
algorithmic description is attributed to the OI Wiki
[maximum bipartite matching article](https://oi-wiki.org/graph/graph-matching/bigraph-match/)
and D. R. Fulkerson, “Note on Dilworth's decomposition theorem for partially
ordered sets,” *Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society* 7(4), 1956,
[DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-1956-0078334-6](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1956-0078334-6).

No source code, prose, figures, or benchmark instances were copied. The
bounded input/output contract, canonical successor-vector tie policy,
workload, oracles, and language ports were authored independently. OI Wiki
content is provided under CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA terms; consult the linked
source for its complete attribution and applicable terms.
