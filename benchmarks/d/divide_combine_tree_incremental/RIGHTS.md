# Provenance and rights

The incremental Divide-and-Combine Tree method is described by [OI Wiki's
English article](https://www.timothywang.ca/OI-wiki/ds/divide-combine/). The
page attributes the underlying construction discussion to Liu Cheng'ao,
“Simple Continuous Segment Data Structure,” WC2019 Camp Exchange, and states
that its contents are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA terms.

This package uses the article to identify the mathematical method: maintain
range-minus-width values with range additions and a minimum query, update
extrema through monotone stacks, and construct the primitive-segment tree
incrementally. No upstream implementation, source code, diagrams, or prose
were copied. The input bound, checksum, workload, references, and all language
ports are independently authored for this repository. This note records
provenance and does not claim to replace or interpret the source page's terms.
