# Rights and provenance

This profile follows the mathematical characterization of transitive reduction
in A. V. Aho, M. R. Garey, and J. D. Ullman, “The Transitive Reduction of a
Directed Graph,” *SIAM Journal on Computing* 1(2), 131–137 (1972),
[DOI 10.1137/0201008](https://doi.org/10.1137/0201008). For a DAG, the
transitive reduction is the unique smallest subgraph preserving reachability;
the reduced edges are exactly those for which no alternate path exists.

The article is used as a mathematical reference only. The ten language ports,
oracles, workload, and documentation are independently authored for this
bounded benchmark; no paper code or prose is copied. The cited publication
retains its own copyright and terms.
