# Rights and provenance

The benchmark is a clean-room implementation of algorithmic ideas described
by Edith Cohen and Haim Kaplan in “Summarizing Data using Bottom-k Sketches,”
§4–5, [DOI 10.1145/1281100.1281133](https://doi.org/10.1145/1281100.1281133),
with Chapter 574 of *Encyclopedia of Algorithms* as the book crosswalk:
[DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_574](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_574).

No source code, book prose, figures, publisher examples, or upstream test
fixtures are reproduced. The ports, references, and fixtures were authored
independently for this bounded integer profile.
