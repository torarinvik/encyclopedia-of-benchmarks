# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is based on the general-graph random-walk method described by
[OI Wiki](https://oi-wiki.org/graph/graph-random-walk/) and the IOI 2019
national-team paper [A discussion of random walks on graph models](https://rusunoi.github.io/books/National-Team-Thesis/2019.pdf).
The OI Wiki page is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA terms; the paper
is cited as an independent mathematical source. Neither source's code,
fixtures, task text, or prose is included here.

All benchmark code, references, contract wording, and fixtures in this package
are newly authored from the mathematical method. No upstream implementation,
comments, examples, or tests were copied.
