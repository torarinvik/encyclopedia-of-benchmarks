# Rights and provenance

This benchmark follows the Alpha Skip Search algorithm described by Charras,
Lecroq, and Pehoushek in their 1998 paper and in [Lecroq's algorithm
reference](https://igm.univ-mlv.fr/~lecroq/string/node33.html). The sources
were used for algorithm ideas only. No code, prose, problem statement, or
examples were copied; the benchmark implementation, contract, and fixtures
are independently authored. The algorithm is not claimed as original.
