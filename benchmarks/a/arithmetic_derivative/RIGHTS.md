The benchmark uses the mathematical definition from E. J. Barbeau, “Remarks on an Arithmetic Derivative,” *Canadian Mathematical Bulletin* 4(2) (1961), 117–122, [DOI 10.4153/CMB-1961-013-0](https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-1961-013-0). The RosettaCodeData [popular-tasks title index](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/main/popular-tasks) served only as a discovery source.

Only the mathematical operation and defining identities are used. No Rosetta task text, implementation, examples, tests, or fixtures were copied. The contract, ports, workload, and references are original to this repository.
