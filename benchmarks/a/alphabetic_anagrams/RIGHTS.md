# Rights and provenance

“Alphabetic Anagrams” was used as a public title for candidate discovery only.
The mathematical method follows the generic multiset permutation count and
lexicographic ranking discussion in Knuth's *The Art of Computer Programming*,
Volume 4A, §7.2.1.2, together with the multinomial count documented by the
[NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, §26.16](https://dlmf.nist.gov/26.16).
Knuth's official [TAOCP publication page](https://cs.stanford.edu/~knuth/taocp.html)
identifies the volume and section.

The bounded scalar contract, multinomial-rank implementation, independent
permutation enumerator, workload, documentation, and ten ports are clean-room
work. No Codewars problem text, examples, tests, solutions, or submissions were
read or copied.
