# Rights and provenance

This benchmark is a clean-room implementation of the Average Rate (AVR)
speed-scaling rule described by Bansal, Bunde, Chan, and Pruhs in [Average
Rate Speed Scaling](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-009-9379-z), and listed in
Kirk Pruhs's 2016 [Encyclopedia of Algorithms chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_390).
Only mathematical algorithm descriptions were consulted. No source code,
examples, fixtures, problem statements, or prose were copied. The bounded
contract, workload, references, and language ports are independently authored.
