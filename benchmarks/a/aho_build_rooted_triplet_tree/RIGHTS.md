# Rights and provenance

The mathematical procedure is based on the published BUILD/Aho algorithm in
Aho, Sagiv, Szymanski, and Ullman, “Inferring a Tree from Lowest Common
Ancestors with an Application to the Optimization of Relational Expressions,”
*SIAM Journal on Computing* 10(3), 1981, pp. 405–421,
[DOI 10.1137/0210030](https://doi.org/10.1137/0210030). The encyclopedia
crosswalk is Jesper Jansson and Wing-Kin Sung, “Algorithms for Combining
Rooted Triplets into a Galled Phylogenetic Network,” Chapter 92,
[DOI 10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_92](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_92).

The algorithm ideas and citations are attributed to those sources. Every
language implementation, oracle, fixture, serialization convention, and
explanatory text in this package is newly written. No source code, examples,
tests, or text have been copied. No license is asserted for the papers or
their publisher-hosted materials; this package makes no claim of ownership of
the underlying published algorithm.
