# Rights and provenance

This benchmark independently implements the complete recursive
minimum-spanning-forest algorithm described by David R. Karger, Philip N.
Klein, and Robert E. Tarjan in “A Randomized Linear-Time Algorithm to Find
Minimum Spanning Trees,” *Journal of the ACM* 42(2), 321–328 (1995),
[doi:10.1145/201019.201022](https://doi.org/10.1145/201019.201022).

The paper is an algorithmic reference only. No source code, benchmark code,
fixtures, or prose has been copied. The bounded contract, pseudorandom sampling
profile, references, workload, and language ports are independently authored.
