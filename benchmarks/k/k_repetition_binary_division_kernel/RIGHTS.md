# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is based on the mathematical procedure in Kolpakov and
Kucherov's “Finding Approximate Repetitions under Hamming Distance” and its
Chapter 24 treatment in the *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*. The sources are
copyrighted and do not grant a source-code license. This package re-expresses
the algorithm from its published descriptions; it does not copy code, prose,
figures, or example data. The bounded input restrictions, output convention,
fixtures, references, and all ten language implementations were authored for
this repository. No third-party source code is included.
