# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation of the colexicographic
fixed-size-subset rank and unrank procedures. The algorithm is specified from
Kreher and Stinson, *Combinatorial Algorithms: Generation, Enumeration, and
Search*, §2.3.2, Algorithms 2.9–2.10. The binomial rank formula and greedy
unranking are cross-checked against Genitrini and Pepin, “Unranking Small
Combinations of a Large Set in Co-Lexicographic Order,” §2.1.

The cited book is copyrighted. No text, pseudocode, code, examples, or
fixtures from it are reproduced. The secondary source is consulted for
algorithmic behavior and not copied. All implementations and workload cases
are newly written.
