# Rights and provenance

The algorithm idea comes from David Arthur and Sergei Vassilvitskii, “k-means++:
The Advantages of Careful Seeding,” *Proceedings of the Eighteenth Annual
ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms* (SODA 2007), pp. 1027–1035. The
authors' paper is available at
[Stanford](https://theory.stanford.edu/~sergei/papers/kMeansPP-soda.pdf), and
the bibliographic record is listed by
[Google Research](https://research.google/pubs/k-means-the-advantages-of-careful-seeding/).

The RosettaGit [public task-title index](https://rosettagit.org/tasks/) supplied
the discovery title “K-means++ clustering”; the index was used only as an
algorithm-title catalog. No task prose, examples, tests, submitted
implementations, or code were copied. The deterministic draw-tape contract,
primary and independent references, workload, and ten language ports were
written independently for this repository.
