# Rights and provenance

The implementations use only the mathematical affine maps defining OEIS
A005658. The sequence definition was cross-referenced against MathWorld and
the bibliographic work of D. A. Klarner and R. Rado; no upstream program,
task text, example, or test was copied. RosettaCodeData supplied title
discovery only.

The local workload and references were independently authored for this
repository. The package is an algorithm benchmark and makes no claim about
ownership of the mathematical sequence.
