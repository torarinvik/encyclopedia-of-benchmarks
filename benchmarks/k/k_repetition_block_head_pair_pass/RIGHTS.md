# Rights and provenance

The package is a clean-room benchmark implementation. It uses the algorithmic
ideas and definitions in Kolpakov and Kucherov's Chapter 24 and §3 paper
references listed in [README.md](README.md), without copying source code,
published example strings, or fixture data. The bounded input domain, byte ABI,
deterministic output ordering, workload fixtures, and all implementations were
written for this repository.

The cited publications remain subject to their respective rights and licenses.
No third-party implementation is included or linked as a runtime dependency.
