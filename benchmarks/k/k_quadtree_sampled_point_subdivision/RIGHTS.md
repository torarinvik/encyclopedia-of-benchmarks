# Rights and provenance

Algorithm attribution is to H. Haverkort, M. McGranaghan, and L. Toma,
“An Edge Quadtree for External Memory,” §2 and §3 (SEA 2013),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-38527-8_12), with Chapter 585 of the
[Encyclopedia of Algorithms](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_585)
as the book crosswalk.

The paper is cited for the every-kth Z-order sample and adjacent-sample
canonical-square split rule. This package's fixed integer frame and direct
serialization of deduplicated `(depth,prefix)` split squares are a bounded
benchmark contract. The paper and book are not treated as a license grant.
No source code, comments, prose, diagrams, examples, or tests were copied.
The ten language ports and two references were independently written from the
published algorithm description.
