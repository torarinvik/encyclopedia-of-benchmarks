# Rights and provenance

This package contains a clean-room implementation of a bounded algorithmic
profile. Its design is informed by the method descriptions in Chapter 24 of
*Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Kolpakov–Kucherov's INRIA RR-4163 (2001), §3,
and Kolpakov–Kucherov's FOCS 1999 paper, §4. No source code, published
examples, or test cases were copied. Workload values and expected byte strings
were authored specifically for this package.

The package does not include third-party code or data. The named algorithms,
mathematical methods, titles, and bibliographic identifiers remain attributed
to their respective authors and publishers. This notice describes provenance;
it does not claim ownership of the underlying methods or publication rights.
