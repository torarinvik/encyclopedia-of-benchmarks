# Rights and provenance

Wang and Cheng, [“Truss Decomposition in Massive Networks”](https://doi.org/10.14778/2311906.2311909),
is the primary algorithm-description source. The paper defines k-truss using
edge triangle support and describes edge-peeling decomposition. The GBBS
[overview](https://paralg.github.io/gbbs/docs/benchmarks/overview/) and
[k-Truss page](https://paralg.github.io/gbbs/docs/benchmarks/substructure/k_truss/)
were used to confirm the benchmark title and its current work-in-progress
status. No third-party source code or prose was reused.

The bounded input/output contract, ten ports, two references, workload
fixtures, and documentation were written independently for this repository.
The cited paper and GBBS documentation retain their respective rights and
licenses; this package does not claim or transfer them. The repository's
project-level licensing terms apply to the original package material.
