# Rights and provenance

The chapter “Planar Geometric Spanners,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*,
Chapter 294, and Kanj and Perković’s “On Geometric Spanners of Euclidean and
Unit Disk Graphs,” §2.3, Figure 3, supplied algorithm descriptions and
bibliographic context. They are cited at DOI
`10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_294` and
`10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2008.1320`. This package contains
independently written code, references, fixtures, and documentation. It does
not reproduce source code, example data, prose, or figures, and it does not
assert a license for the source publications. The STACS 2008 paper states a
Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivs license for the paper; this benchmark
uses only the mathematical method and citation, not redistributed paper
content. See the source references in [README.md](README.md).
