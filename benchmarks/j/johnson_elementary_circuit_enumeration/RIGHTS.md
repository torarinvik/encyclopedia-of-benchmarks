# Rights and provenance

This benchmark is a clean-room implementation of the elementary-circuit
enumeration algorithm described by Donald B. Johnson in [his 1975
paper](https://doi.org/10.1137/0204007). Only the mathematical procedure and
its complexity statement were consulted. No source code, examples, fixtures,
problem statements, or prose were copied. The bounded contract, workload,
references, and language ports are independently authored.
