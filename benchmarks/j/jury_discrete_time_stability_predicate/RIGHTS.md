# Rights and provenance

The ten native ports and the two references are clean-room implementations of
the stated Jury-table and Schur–Cohn criteria. RosettaCodeData supplied title
discovery only. No task prose, source code, examples, or tests were reused.
Workload vectors and expected scalar values were selected and derived for this
bounded profile.

Mathematical sources:

- E. I. Jury and J. Blanchard, “A Stability Test for Linear Discrete Systems
  in Table Form,” *Proceedings of the IRE* 49 (1961), 1947–1948,
  [original issue scan](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-IRE/60s/IRE-Proceedings-1961-12-R.pdf).
- E. I. Jury, “A Simplified Stability Criterion for Linear Discrete Systems,”
  *Proceedings of the IRE* 50 (1962), 1493–1500,
  [DOI 10.1109/JRPROC.1962.288193](https://doi.org/10.1109/JRPROC.1962.288193).
- K. S. Choo, “An elementary proof of the Jury test for real polynomials,”
  *Automatica* 47(1) (2011),
  [DOI 10.1016/j.automatica.2010.10.040](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2010.10.040).
- H. Dym and N. Young, “A Schur–Cohn theorem for matrix polynomials,”
  *Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society* 33 (1990), 337–366,
  [DOI 10.1017/S0013091500004806](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0013091500004806).
- RosettaCodeData, pinned [popular-task index](https://github.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/blob/1d475861d7141ef2fafff293d9339f56e4b0f5db/popular-tasks),
  title discovery only.

No third-party implementation is included.
