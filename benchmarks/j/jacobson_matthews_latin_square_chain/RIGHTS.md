# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Mark T. Jacobson and Peter Matthews,
“Generating uniformly distributed random latin squares,” *Journal of
Combinatorial Designs* 4(6), 405–437 (1996),
[doi:10.1002/(SICI)1520-6610(1996)4:6<405::AID-JCD3>3.0.CO;2-J](https://doi.org/10.1002/%28SICI%291520-6610%281996%294%3A6%3C405%3A%3AAID-JCD3%3E3.0.CO%3B2-J).
The transition rules were cross-checked against ETH Zürich, *Random Algorithms
II*, FS 2015, Section 2.9.2,
[lecture notes PDF](https://cadmo.ethz.ch/education/lectures/FS15/rand_alg_2/ETHSkript.pdf).

Only the mathematical algorithmic description was consulted. The bounded
input and output contract, deterministic choice tape, implementations,
independent reference, fixtures, and explanatory prose are clean-room work.
No source code, prose, figures, or examples from the paper, notes, or
RosettaGit are reproduced. RosettaGit was consulted only as a title index for
discovery. Rights in the cited works remain with their authors and publishers.
