# Rights and provenance

The algorithm choice and recurrence are informed by the [OI Wiki Zhouge sieve
article](https://oi-wiki.org/math/number-theory/zhou/) and Ren Zhizhou's
“Several Methods for Summing Multiplicative Functions,” in the 2016 Chinese
National Olympiad in Informatics training-team paper collection
([PDF](https://yutong.site/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/%E5%9B%BD%E5%AE%B6%E9%9B%86%E8%AE%AD%E9%98%9F2016%E8%AE%BA%E6%96%87%E9%9B%86.pdf)). The OI Wiki article is published under CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA terms. The cited paper remains under its authors' and publisher's rights.

This package uses algorithmic ideas and recurrence relationships only. The
ten language ports, optimized and independent references, workload, and
metadata were written for this repository. No source code, explanatory prose,
figures, or example inputs were copied from the references.
