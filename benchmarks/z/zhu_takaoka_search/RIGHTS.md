# Rights and provenance

The benchmark implements the Zhu–Takaoka string-search method from its
mathematical description. Algorithm identification uses [NIST DADS](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/zhuTakaoka.html), the [Charras–Lecroq description](https://monge.univ-mlv.fr/~lecroq/string/node20.html), and the bibliographic record for Zhu and Takaoka's [1987 paper](https://ipsj.ixsq.nii.ac.jp/records/59825).

Only the algorithm name, facts about its shifts and complexity, and citation
metadata are used. No reference implementation, source code, examples, tests,
or source prose are reproduced. All ports, workload fixtures, and reference
models are independently authored.
