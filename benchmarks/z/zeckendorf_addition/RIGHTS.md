# Rights and provenance

The pinned [RosettaCodeData popular-task title index](https://github.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/blob/1d475861d7141ef2fafff293d9339f56e4b0f5db/popular-tasks)
was used only to discover the title “Zeckendorf arithmetic.” No Rosetta task
text, code, examples, or tests were copied.

The algorithmic method is based on Connor Ahlbach, Jeremy Usatine, Christiane
Frougny, and Nicholas Pippenger, “Efficient Algorithms for Zeckendorf
Arithmetic,” *The Fibonacci Quarterly* 51(3), 2013, pp. 248–254. The primary
source is available at the [journal PDF](https://www.fq.math.ca/Papers1/51-3/AhlbachUsatineFrougnyPippenger.pdf)
and [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00150517.2013.12427944); its preprint is
[arXiv:1207.4497](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4497). The ports and both
references are clean-room implementations of the stated finite rewrite
procedure and an independently structured decode-and-encode oracle.

The article remains subject to its publisher's rights. Citation here does not
grant rights to reproduce the article. This package contains original code,
fixtures, and explanatory wording; it does not include the paper or copied
task material.
