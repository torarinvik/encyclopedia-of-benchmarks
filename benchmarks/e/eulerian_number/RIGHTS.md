# Rights and provenance

The OI Wiki “Eulerian Number” navigation heading served as the algorithm
discovery lead. Its license notice lists CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA. This package
uses only the standard mathematical definition and recurrence as algorithm
ideas. No OI Wiki source code, prose, worked examples, or tests are copied.

Independent mathematical references are Stanley, *Enumerative
Combinatorics*, Volume 1, §1.4, and the Eulerian triangle in
[OEIS A008292](https://oeis.org/A008292). The contract, ten ports, references,
and fixtures are written independently for this repository.
