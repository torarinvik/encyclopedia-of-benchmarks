# Rights and attribution

This package is a clean-room implementation of the geometric problem of finding the two supporting tangent vertices from a point outside a strictly convex polygon. Its algorithmic direction is attributed to the [Codeforces tutorial](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/140112), and its benchmark provenance to the [Library Checker proposal](https://github.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/issues/1371).

No redistribution license for either referenced page is asserted. The implementations contain no copied source code, examples, or extended prose. They encode the stated mathematical operation under the bounded integer-coordinate contract in `workload.json`. Keep these references with the package when redistributing it.
