[Euler Tour Tree article](https://en.oi-wiki.org/ds/ett/). The article states
that OI Wiki content is available under CC BY-SA 4.0 and SATA terms. OI Wiki is

treap representation, workload, fixtures, primary and independent oracles,
documentation, and ten language ports were independently written. No
upstream implementation or article prose was copied. Local artifacts follow
