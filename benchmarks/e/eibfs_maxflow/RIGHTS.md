# Rights and provenance

The algorithm reference is Goldberg, Hed, Kaplan, Kohli, Tarjan, and Werneck,
[“Faster and More Dynamic Maximum Flow by Incremental Breadth-First Search,”
ESA 2015](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/faster-and-more-dynamic-maximum-flow-incremental-breadth-first-search/)
([official paper PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/ghkktw_ESA2015.pdf)).
The paper is used as an algorithmic idea reference. No implementation code or
paper prose is copied. The ports, oracles, fixtures, and documentation are
written independently for this repository.

The benchmark itself is original clean-room material in this repository. The
authors' paper copyright and publication terms remain with their respective
owners.
