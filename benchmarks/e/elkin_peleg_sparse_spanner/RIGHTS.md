# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation based on algorithm descriptions
in the *Encyclopedia of Algorithms* chapter “Sparse Graph Spanners,” DOI
10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_387, and the cited paper by Michael Elkin and David
Peleg, “(1 + ε, β)-Spanner Constructions for General Graphs,” DOI
10.1137/S0097539701393384. The references are used to identify the problem,
construction stages, parameter relationships, and guarantees.

No book or paper code, pseudocode, prose, figures, examples, or test data have
been copied. The ported implementations, canonical tie rules, bounded framing,
workload, and independent references were written for this repository. The
source publications retain their respective copyrights and licenses; this
package does not redistribute either publication.
