# Rights and provenance

The Fabergé egg-drop title was used for public-title candidate discovery only.
The generic method follows Michael Boardman's “The Egg-Drop Numbers,”
*Mathematics Magazine* 77(5), 2004, pp. 368–372,
[doi:10.1080/0025570X.2004.11953281](https://doi.org/10.1080/0025570X.2004.11953281).
The publisher's [JSTOR stable record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3219201) identifies
the same article and issue.
The article defines the maximum-floor capacity for a fixed egg and move budget,
gives its recurrence, and derives its partial-binomial expression.

The bounded scalar contract, in-place recurrence implementation, independent
binomial-count reference, workload, documentation, and ten ports are clean-room
work. No Codewars statement, examples, tests, solutions, or submissions were
read or copied.
