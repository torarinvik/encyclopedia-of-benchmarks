# Rights and provenance

The *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, Chapter 172, “Graph Isomorphism,” is the
algorithm-description source for the layered reduction of edge-colored graphs
to vertex-colored graphs. This benchmark uses the chapter's color-bit layer
idea and its allowed path connection for copies of each original vertex.

The package implementation, bounded input/output contract, references,
fixtures, and language ports are clean-room work. The chapter was used for
algorithm attribution and method specification only. No code, prose, figures,
examples, or test data were copied. No upstream license or permission is
asserted for the book's content.
