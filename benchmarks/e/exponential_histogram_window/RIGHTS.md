# Provenance

The algorithmic idea is from Mayur Datar, Aristides Gionis, Piotr Indyk, and
Rajeev Motwani, [*Maintaining Stream Statistics over Sliding Windows*](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spr04/cos598B/bib/DatarGIM.pdf).
This package independently authors its code, contract, and fixtures; no source
text or examples are reused. The source's reuse terms have not been relied on.
