# Rights and provenance

The Euclid–Mullin recurrence and its initial terms are described by Booker and
Irvine, “The Euclid–Mullin Graph,” *Journal of Number Theory* 165 (2016),
30–57: <https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/files/65358793/emgraph.pdf>.
The sequence originates in Mullin's 1963 note, “Recursive Function Theory (A
Modern Look at a Euclidean Idea),” *Bulletin of the American Mathematical
Society* 69, 737: <https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1963-11017-4>. OEIS A000945
is cited as a sequence cross-reference: <https://oeis.org/A000945>.

RosettaCodeData supplied title discovery only:
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/main/popular-tasks>.
No task prose, examples, tests, or code were reused. The bounded profile,
ten language ports, references, and workload were independently authored for
this repository.
