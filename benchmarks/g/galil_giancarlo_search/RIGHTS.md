# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is attributed to Zvi Galil and Raffaele Giancarlo, “On the Exact
Complexity of String Matching: Upper Bounds,” *SIAM Journal on Computing*
21(3), 407–437 (1992),
[doi:10.1137/0221028](https://doi.org/10.1137/0221028). The search-phase
transition was cross-checked against Thierry Lecroq's
[Galil–Giancarlo algorithm description](https://igm.univ-mlv.fr/~lecroq/string/node11.html).

Only mathematical algorithm descriptions were consulted. The implementation,
bounded contract, direct and independent references, workload, and prose are
clean-room work. No source code, prose, figures, or examples are reproduced.
Rights in the cited work remain with its authors and publisher.
