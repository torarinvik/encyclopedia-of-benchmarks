# Rights and provenance

The algorithm is identified from Chapter 340, “Regular Expression Matching,”
in *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, second edition (Springer, 2016), and from
Gonzalo Navarro and Mathieu Raffinot, “New Techniques for Regular Expression
Searching,” *Algorithmica* 41 (2005), 89–116,
[doi:10.1007/s00453-004-1123-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-004-1123-2).
The paper describes Glushkov position automata and bit-parallel regular
expression searching.

Only the mathematical algorithmic idea was consulted. All package code,
fixtures, and explanatory prose here are independently written. No book or
paper source code, prose, figures, or examples are included. The package is a
bounded clean-room implementation; source rights remain with the cited
authors/publisher.
