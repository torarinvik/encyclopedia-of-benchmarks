# Rights and provenance

The graph operation, simple-graph semantics, and positive weight range are
identified by the [Library Checker General Weighted Matching task](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/master/graph/general_weighted_matching/task.md)
and [limits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/master/graph/general_weighted_matching/info.toml).
The task repository is licensed under [Apache-2.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yosupo06/library-checker-problems/master/LICENSE);
no problem text, examples, tests, or upstream implementation are reproduced here.

The algorithm is attributed to Edmonds' weighted blossom method as presented
by Korte and Vygen, [*Combinatorial Optimization*, Chapter 11](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-21711-5_11).
Only the mathematical method is used. All port source, oracle code, contract
wording, and workload fixtures are independently authored; no code or prose is
copied.
