# Source and rights

This package is a clean-room implementation of the moment-curve graph-drawing construction described by David R. Wood, “Three-Dimensional Graph Drawing,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_656`.

The chapter and cited papers remain copyrighted. No source code, prose, or figures are copied, and no upstream source-code license is asserted. Preserve the bibliographic attribution above when redistributing the benchmark.
