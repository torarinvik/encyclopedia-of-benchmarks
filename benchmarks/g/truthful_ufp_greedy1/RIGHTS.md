# Rights and attribution

This package is a clean-room implementation of Greedy-1 as specified in Piotr Krysta and Berthold Vöcking, “Utilitarian Mechanism Design for Single-Minded Agents,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_454`, Figure 2 and its unsplittable-flow definitions. The chapter text and figure are copyrighted; no source-code license is asserted. This package derives its code from the mathematical recurrence and independently selected bounds, input encoding, tie rules, and workload. No chapter prose or figure artwork is included.
