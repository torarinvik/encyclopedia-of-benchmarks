# Rights and provenance

The Fortunate-number definition is used as an algorithmic fact from Eric Weisstein, [“Fortunate Prime,” MathWorld](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/FortunatePrime.html), and OEIS [A005235](https://oeis.org/A005235). The references state the relation between primorials, the next prime after the Euclid number, and the least positive offset `m>1`.

Only the mathematical definition and the published initial sequence values are used. No source code, task statements, examples, or tests are copied. The contract, trial-division implementation, segmented-sieve reference, workload framing, and ten language ports are clean-room work. The cited web resources retain their own copyright and terms; this repository does not redistribute their content.
