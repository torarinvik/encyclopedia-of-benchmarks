# Source and reuse

The [CP-Algorithms article on fixed-length paths](https://cp-algorithms.com/graph/fixed_length_paths.html)
identifies the adjacency-matrix power method. Its text is licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0. This package uses the mathematical idea and independently
authored contracts, implementations and fixtures; no article text, code or
examples are copied. The article is not an upstream benchmark attribution.
