# Source and reuse

The [CP-Algorithms article on fixed-length paths](https://cp-algorithms.com/graph/fixed_length_paths.html)
identifies the min-plus matrix-power method. Its text is licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0; this package uses the mathematical idea and independently authored
contracts, implementations and fixtures. No article text, code or examples are
copied. The article is not a claim that this workload is an upstream benchmark.
