# Rights and provenance

The mathematical algorithm is based on Pedro F. Felzenszwalb and Daniel P.
Huttenlocher, “Distance Transforms of Sampled Functions,” Cornell Computing
and Information Science Technical Report TR2004-1963 (2004), available from
the authors' Cornell page at
<https://www.cs.cornell.edu/dph/papers/dt.pdf>.

This package uses the sampled-function objective and lower-envelope method
only. The integer bounds, tie policy, ABI, fixtures, oracle implementations,
ports, and prose are original benchmark material. No source code, paper
prose, figures, or examples are reproduced.
