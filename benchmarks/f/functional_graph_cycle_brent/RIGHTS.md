# Source and reuse

The benchmark uses the mathematical idea of Brent's cycle-finding method as
described in the [formal cycle-finding study](https://ticamore.logic.at/publications/ITP_2018_paper_16.pdf)
and the [CP-Algorithms factorization discussion](https://cp-algorithms.com/algebra/factorization.html).
CP-Algorithms text is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; no source text, code, or fixtures
were copied. The benchmark contract, implementations, and workload are authored
independently.
