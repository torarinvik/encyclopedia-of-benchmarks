# Rights and provenance

The algorithm profile is based on Bengt Fornberg, “Generation of Finite
Difference Formulas on Arbitrarily Spaced Grids,” *Mathematics of
Computation* 51(184), 1988, pp. 699–706,
[DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-1988-0935077-0](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1988-0935077-0).
The recurrence is also available in the [author-hosted paper
PDF](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/sites/default/files/attached-files/mathcomp_88_fd_formulas.pdf).
NIST DLMF [§3.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.4) is cited for the surrounding
finite-difference subject area, not as the source of Fornberg's arbitrary-grid
recurrence.

The paper is copyrighted by the American Mathematical Society. No license for
the paper is asserted. The benchmark contains a clean-room expression of the
mathematical recurrence, newly authored finite-field input/output framing,
independent moment-system reference, and hand-derived fixtures. It reuses no
source code, comments, tests, prose, or published coefficient tables.
