# Rights and provenance

The package is based on the algorithmic specification in NASA Ames Research Center technical report RNR-94-007, “The NAS Parallel Benchmarks” (March 1994), FT kernel section. The ports are clean-room implementations derived from the published mathematical recurrence. No upstream source code, comments, tabular values, or report prose are copied into this package. No third-party source license is asserted for the new files.

Reference: [NASA RNR-94-007 PDF](https://www.nas.nasa.gov/assets/nas/pdf/techreports/1994/rnr-94-007.pdf).
