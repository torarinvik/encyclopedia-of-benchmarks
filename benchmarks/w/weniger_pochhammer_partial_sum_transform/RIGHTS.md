# Rights and provenance

The mathematical transformation follows the coefficient choice in NIST DLMF
[§3.9(v), Eq. 3.9.16](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.9#E16), together with the
weighted partial-sum transform in Eq. 3.9.13. The benchmark contract, integer
product-clearing form, references, fixtures, and language ports are written
independently.

No source code, comments, prose, examples, or tests have been copied. The
DLMF's terms govern its text and presentation; this package makes no claim of
a license for copied material.
