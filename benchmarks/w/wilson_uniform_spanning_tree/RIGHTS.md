# Rights and provenance

The method description is based on David B. Wilson, “Generating Random
Spanning Trees More Quickly than the Cover Time,” STOC 1996, DOI
[10.1145/237814.237880](https://doi.org/10.1145/237814.237880), especially
Figure 1, `RandomTreeWithRoot`, in the [author-hosted paper](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~15859n/RelatedWork/RandomTrees-Wilson.pdf).
The paper is copyrighted by ACM.

Only the algorithm's mathematical behavior is used. No code, pseudocode
text, comments, examples, fixtures, or figures are copied or adapted. The
benchmark's bounded graph/tape contract, workload, references, and ten ports
are independently authored. Caller-supplied draw tapes make runs
reproducible and do not claim statistical randomness or uniformity for any
particular fixture.
