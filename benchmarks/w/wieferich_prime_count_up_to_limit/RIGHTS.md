# Rights and provenance

The benchmark uses the mathematical condition and known small values from Richard Crandall, Karl Dilcher, and Carl Pomerance, “A Search for Wieferich and Wilson Primes,” *Mathematics of Computation* 66(217) (1997), 433–449: https://www.reed.edu/physics/faculty/crandall/papers/S0025-5718-97-00791-6.pdf. The RosettaCodeData popular-tasks index served only as a title-discovery source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/main/popular-tasks.

Only the congruence definition and cited mathematical values are used. No task prose, source code, examples, tests, or fixtures were copied. The bounded contract, native ports, references, and workload are original to this repository.
