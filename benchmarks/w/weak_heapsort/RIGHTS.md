# Rights and provenance

This benchmark follows the weak-heapsort algorithm described in [NIST's
Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures](https://xlinux.nist.gov/dads/HTML/weakheapsort.html)
and R. D. Dutton's 1993 paper, [“Weak-Heap Sort”](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01990520).
These sources were used for algorithm ideas only. The benchmark code,
contract, and fixtures are independently authored; no source code, prose,
or examples were reused. The algorithm is not claimed as original.
