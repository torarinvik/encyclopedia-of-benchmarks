# Rights and provenance

The package contains clean-room implementations of the bounded exact numerator computation for `H_n^(2)`. No source code, prose, tests, or examples were copied from external projects.

The mathematical definition and sequence cross-checks come from Eric Weisstein, [“Wolstenholme Number,” MathWorld](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/WolstenholmeNumber.html), and [OEIS A007406](https://oeis.org/A007406). The references and workload were independently authored. External sources are cited for mathematical attribution and verification of the published sequence only.
