# Rights and provenance

The ten ports and two reference implementations are clean-room descriptions
of the Routh–Hurwitz algorithms. The pinned RosettaCodeData index supplied
title discovery only. No Rosetta task source, prose, examples, or tests were
copied. All workload inputs and expected scalar results are original
hand-derived fixtures.

Mathematical and historical sources:

- E. J. Routh, *A Treatise on the Stability of a Given State of Motion*
  (1877), [Internet Archive record](https://archive.org/details/treatiseonstabil00routuoft).
- A. Hurwitz, “Über die Bedingungen, unter welchen eine Gleichung nur Wurzeln
  mit negativen reellen Theilen besitzt,” *Mathematische Annalen* 46 (1895),
  273–284, [DOI 10.1007/BF01446812](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01446812).
- [Encyclopedia of Mathematics: Routh–Hurwitz criterion](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Routh-Hurwitz_criterion).
- RosettaCodeData, pinned [popular-tasks index](https://github.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/blob/1d475861d7141ef2fafff293d9339f56e4b0f5db/popular-tasks),
  title discovery only.

The Routh reference supplies the primary exact-table profile; the Hurwitz
criterion supplies the independent determinant formulation. No third-party
implementation is included.
