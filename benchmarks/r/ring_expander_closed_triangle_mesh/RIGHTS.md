# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic source is the Ring-Expander traversal in Gurung, Luffel,
Lindstrom, and Rossignac, “LR: Compact Connectivity Representation for
Triangle Meshes,” *ACM Transactions on Graphics* 30(4), Article 67 (2011),
§4.2, DOI [10.1145/1964921.1964962](https://doi.org/10.1145/1964921.1964962).
Chapter 589 of the user-supplied *Encyclopedia of Algorithms* is the chapter
crosswalk entry. The paper is used for algorithmic ideas and operator
definitions only.

All ports, references, contract text, and fixtures in this package were
written independently. No source code, paper prose, mesh data, examples, or
tests were copied. The supplied seed and bounded connected closed-manifold
validation are benchmark-profile choices that make one traversal deterministic
and total over the accepted domain.

The paper and book remain under their respective rights. This note does not
grant rights to redistribute either source.
