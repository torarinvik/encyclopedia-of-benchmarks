# Rights and provenance

The algorithmic method is the finite-rotation vector identity for a rotation about a fixed axis. The historical source is Hui Cheng and K. C. Gupta, “An Historical Note on Finite Rotations,” *Journal of Applied Mechanics* 56 (1989), 139–142, [PDF](https://iel.ucdavis.edu/publication/1989/j_ASMEAM.pdf). Its Eq. 1 presents the vector form and attributes the finite-rotation formula historically to Euler. The matrix form is independently stated in Eric Weisstein, [“Rodrigues' Rotation Formula,” MathWorld](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/RodriguesRotationFormula.html).

The paper and MathWorld page are cited as algorithmic references only. No code, prose, examples, figures, or tests were copied. The package contract, references, workload, and ten language implementations were authored independently.
