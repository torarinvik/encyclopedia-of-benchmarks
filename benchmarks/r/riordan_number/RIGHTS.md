# Rights and provenance

All ports and fixtures are original implementations of the Riordan recurrence
and the central-trinomial difference identity. These mathematical formulas
and sequence definitions are not copied source code. No task code, prose, or
fixtures were taken from RosettaCodeData.

The path definition and recurrence are documented by Chen, Deng, and Yang,
“Riordan Paths and Derangements.” The independent identity is from Callan,
“Riordan Numbers Are Differences of Trinomial Coefficients.”

- [Chen, Deng, and Yang](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0602298)
- [Callan's note](https://pages.stat.wisc.edu/~callan/notes/riordan/riordan.pdf)
- [OEIS A005043](https://oeis.org/A005043)
- [RosettaCodeData popular-tasks index](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acmeism/RosettaCodeData/main/popular-tasks)
