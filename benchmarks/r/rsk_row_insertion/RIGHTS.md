examples, or tests. The package supplies its own bounded contract, encoding,
fixtures, and implementations.

The repository contains a separate `hook_length_tableaux` profile for counting
standard tableaux of a supplied shape; this RSK profile constructs the insertion
and recording tableaux from a permutation and is algorithmically distinct.
