# Rights and provenance

The package is a clean-room implementation of public mathematical ideas:
the Heffter–Edmonds–Ringel rotation principle, Euler's formula for orientable
surface embeddings, and Wagner's forbidden-minor characterization of planar
graphs. References:

- Rotation systems and Euler characteristic: https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/250c3d31-3080-41fe-a9ae-f5dd1ea7537c/content
- Face walks over darts: https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/teaching/comptop/2023/notes/09-planar-graphs.html
- Wagner's theorem: https://kam.mff.cuni.cz/~kyncl/tgg/ggt02.pdf

No source code or prose is copied. The contract, fixtures, references, and
language ports are independently written. Consult the cited works and their
hosting institutions for rights applicable to those documents.
