# Rights and provenance

This package is a clean-room implementation of the `RingCoveringExact` mathematical procedure described in Paola Flocchini, “Uniform Covering of Rings and Lines by Memoryless Mobile Sensors,” *Encyclopedia of Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4939-2864-4_594`.

The chapter and its summarized source papers are copyrighted. No source code, figures, or prose are reproduced here. This package asserts no license over the source chapter. The implementation is limited to the documented serial activation trace on a fixed rational lattice.
