# Provenance

The three-way peeling and reverse fingerprint assignment are described by
Thomas Mueller Graf and Daniel Lemire in [*Xor Filters: Faster and Smaller Than
Bloom and Cuckoo Filters*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08258). The bounded
contract and implementation use the algorithmic idea only; no upstream code,
figures, prose, or test data are copied.
