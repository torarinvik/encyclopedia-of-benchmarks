# Rights and provenance

Algorithm source: NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, §3.6(v),
Olver's algorithm, Eqs. 3.6.8–3.6.10:
https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.6.v . The source was consulted for the mathematical
recurrences, cutoff inequality, and back-substitution relation only.

No source code, comments, examples, tests, or prose were copied. The finite
binary64 profile, coefficient bounds, ABI, statuses, workload fixtures, and
all language implementations were independently specified and written for
this benchmark.
